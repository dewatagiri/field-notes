A market falls. How long until it heals?Pasaran jatuh. Berapa lama baru pulih?

A figure often quoted says corrections recover in about 4 to 9.6 months. I checked what it measures against Bursa Malaysia, Japan, Hong Kong and the S&P 500. The cause of the fall decides the wait, not the country.Satu angka yang sering disebut mengatakan pembetulan pasaran pulih dalam kira-kira 4 hingga 9.6 bulan. Saya semak apa sebenarnya yang diukur angka itu pada Bursa Malaysia, Jepun, Hong Kong dan S&P 500. Puncanya yang menentukan tempoh menunggu, bukan negaranya.

Azman Taufik · 23 Nov 2026 · about 14 min readAzman Taufik · 23 Nov 2026 · kira-kira 14 minit membaca
The short versionRingkasan pantas
  • The often-quoted 9.6 months is the average length of a bear market, from peak to low. It is not the time to get your money back.1
  • For the S&P 500, falls of 20% or more took a median 39 months to regain the old high (average 49). Three took 65 to 90 months. These are my calculations.3
  • Nikkei took 410 months. KLCI took 159 after 1994. Hang Seng took about 122 after 2007. KLCI (2014) and Hang Seng (2018) are still below their peaks today.
  • The cause sorts the cases. Shocks to positioning or mood heal in months. Resets (banks, rates, a currency run, new rules for big firms) take years.
  • My test of a 200-month average buy rule won 34 of 36 closed trades on the four markets. Yet drawdowns reached 14% to 57%, and single trades were held for up to 80 months.
  • Applied today (23 Nov 2026): the S&P 500 sell-off answers YES on rates and PARTLY on the rest. Malaysia answers mostly NO or PARTLY, with foreign selling but a firm ringgit.
  • 9.6 bulan yang sering disebut itu ialah purata tempoh pasaran menurun (bear market), dari puncak ke paras terendah. Ia bukan tempoh untuk modal kembali.1
  • Bagi S&P 500, kejatuhan 20% atau lebih mengambil median 39 bulan untuk kembali ke puncak lama (purata 49). Tiga kes mengambil 65 hingga 90 bulan. Ini kiraan saya sendiri.3
  • Nikkei mengambil 410 bulan. KLCI mengambil 159 bulan selepas 1994. Hang Seng kira-kira 122 bulan selepas 2007. KLCI (2014) dan Hang Seng (2018) masih di bawah puncak masing-masing hari ini.
  • Punca yang mengasingkan kes-kes ini. Kejutan kepada kedudukan atau sentimen pulih dalam beberapa bulan. Tetapan semula (bank, kadar faedah, serangan ke atas mata wang, peraturan baharu untuk syarikat besar) mengambil bertahun-tahun.
  • Ujian saya ke atas peraturan beli berasaskan purata 200 bulan menang 34 daripada 36 dagangan yang ditutup di empat pasaran. Namun susut nilai mencecah 14% hingga 57%, dan satu dagangan dipegang sehingga 80 bulan.
  • Diterapkan hari ini (23 Nov 2026): kejatuhan S&P 500 menjawab YA bagi kadar faedah dan SEBAHAGIAN bagi yang lain. Malaysia menjawab kebanyakannya TIDAK atau SEBAHAGIAN, dengan jualan asing tetapi ringgit kukuh.

If you hold shares, you want one number: how long until I am whole again? The usual answer is a few months, and I wanted to see whether that holds outside America. So I pulled the history for four markets and went looking for what each fall was about.

Jika anda memegang saham, anda mahu satu nombor sahaja: berapa lama lagi baru modal saya kembali? Jawapan biasa ialah beberapa bulan, dan saya mahu lihat sama ada ia betul di luar Amerika. Jadi saya ambil sejarah empat pasaran dan mencari apa punca setiap kejatuhan.

Credit first. The 9.6-month figure is Ned Davis Research's, as reported by Kiplinger and Hartford Funds. The idea of buying when the monthly chart drops below its 200-month average I heard from a trader called Oliver. I could not find his rule written down, so the test later in this piece is my own version. The research and the calculations are mine, and I label each one as mine.

Kredit dahulu. Angka 9.6 bulan ialah milik Ned Davis Research, seperti yang dilaporkan oleh Kiplinger dan Hartford Funds. Idea membeli apabila carta bulanan jatuh di bawah purata 200 bulan saya dengar daripada seorang peniaga bernama Oliver. Saya tidak menjumpai peraturannya ditulis, jadi ujian di bahagian kemudian ialah versi saya sendiri. Kajian dan kiraan ini kerja saya, dan setiap satu saya tanda sebagai kiraan saya.

01The 9.6 months measures the wrong thingAngka 9.6 bulan mengukur perkara yang salah

Ned Davis Research counts the average bear market since 1929 at about 9.6 months, from the peak to the low.12 That is the fall. The climb back to the old high comes after, and it is longer. Yardeni puts a full recovery at around 2.5 years on average.22

Ned Davis Research mengira purata pasaran menurun sejak 1929 ialah kira-kira 9.6 bulan, dari puncak ke paras terendah.12 Itu tempoh jatuh. Perjalanan naik semula ke puncak lama datang selepas itu, dan ia lebih panjang. Yardeni meletakkan pemulihan penuh pada sekitar 2.5 tahun secara purata.22

I measured the S&P 500 from the old peak to the month it regained that peak. I used monthly average prices since 1950, so exact peak months can shift a little.3 Seven falls reached 20% or more.

Saya ukur S&P 500 dari puncak lama hingga bulan ia mencapai puncak itu semula. Saya guna harga purata bulanan sejak 1950, jadi bulan puncak yang tepat boleh berbeza sedikit.3 Tujuh kejatuhan mencecah 20% atau lebih.

Even the quick cases did not fit 9.6 months. 1987 took 23 months to regain the high, though the fall itself lasted only four. The 2022 fall took 24. The ones in blue, 1973, 2000 and 2007, took between 65 and 90 months.

Kes yang pantas pun tidak sepadan dengan 9.6 bulan. Tahun 1987 mengambil 23 bulan untuk kembali ke puncak, walaupun kejatuhannya hanya empat bulan. Kejatuhan 2022 mengambil 24 bulan. Tiga kes berwarna biru, 1973, 2000 dan 2007, mengambil antara 65 dan 90 bulan.

The shorter fall-only numbers do exist. Falls of 10% or more averaged 28 months in total (median 21), and 2018, 2020 and 2025 took only 5 to 7 months. So many dips are short. It is wrong to read that as the typical recovery from a serious fall.

Angka jatuh sahaja yang lebih pendek memang wujud. Kejatuhan 10% atau lebih mengambil purata 28 bulan keseluruhan (median 21), dan 2018, 2020 serta 2025 hanya 5 hingga 7 bulan. Jadi banyak penurunan memang singkat. Ia tersasar apabila itu dibaca sebagai tempoh pemulihan biasa selepas kejatuhan serius.

Trap: real versus nominalPerangkap: nilai sebenar dan nilai nominal

These are price numbers before inflation. Adjusted for inflation, the 1968 peak took 277 months to regain, and the 2000 peak took 171. My calculation, same data. A recovery in index points is not a recovery in what the money buys. Dividends are also not counted here, and I have no figure for them.

Angka ini ialah harga sebelum inflasi. Selepas diselaraskan dengan inflasi, puncak 1968 mengambil 277 bulan untuk dicapai semula, dan puncak 2000 mengambil 171 bulan. Kiraan saya, data yang sama. Pulih dari segi mata indeks bukan pulih dari segi apa yang boleh dibeli dengan wang itu. Dividen juga tidak dikira di sini, dan saya tidak mempunyai angkanya.

Why the gap? Not because Asian companies were worse. The next section shows what knocked each market down, and it was not the same kind of event each time.

Mengapa ada jurang? Bukan kerana syarikat Asia lebih lemah. Bahagian seterusnya menunjukkan apa yang menjatuhkan setiap pasaran, dan ia bukan jenis peristiwa yang sama setiap kali.

03What knocked each one downApa yang menjatuhkan setiap pasaran

I sorted each fall by one question: was it a shock to positioning and mood, or a reset of how the economy or the market works? A Reset means something structural changed: bank balance sheets, the rate or inflation regime, a currency run, or new rules for the biggest companies. A Shock means people sold fast, but the system underneath was still standing.

Saya asingkan setiap kejatuhan dengan satu soalan: adakah ia kejutan kepada kedudukan dan sentimen, atau tetapan semula cara ekonomi atau pasaran berfungsi? Tetapan semula (Reset) bermaksud sesuatu yang struktur berubah: kunci kira-kira bank, rejim kadar faedah atau inflasi, serangan ke atas mata wang, atau peraturan baharu untuk syarikat terbesar. Kejutan (Shock) bermaksud orang menjual dengan pantas, tetapi sistem di bawahnya masih tegak.

RESET

Japan, 1990Jepun, 1990

The Nikkei nearly quadrupled in 1985 to 1989. Japan was about 45% of world market value at the peak, against about 6% now.8 The Bank of Japan raised its discount rate five times from May 1989 to August 1990, from 2.5% to 6%.7 The finance ministry also capped property lending. Banks later wrote off about ¥100 trillion.6

Nikkei hampir empat kali ganda dalam 1985 hingga 1989. Jepun ketika puncak kira-kira 45% nilai pasaran dunia, berbanding kira-kira 6% sekarang.8 Bank of Japan menaikkan kadar diskaun lima kali dari Mei 1989 hingga Ogos 1990, daripada 2.5% kepada 6%.7 Kementerian kewangan turut mengehadkan pinjaman hartanah. Bank kemudian menghapus kira-kira ¥100 trilion.6

Wait: 410 months.Tempoh menunggu: 410 bulan.

RESET

Malaysia, 1997 to 98Malaysia, 1997 hingga 98

The baht floated on 2 July 1997. Bank Negara stopped defending the ringgit on 14 July. The ringgit went from about 2.42 per US dollar in April 1997 to 4.88 in January 1998. The KLSE index fell from above 1,300 to 262.7. Capital controls came in September 1998.9

Baht diapungkan pada 2 Julai 1997. Bank Negara berhenti mempertahankan ringgit pada 14 Julai. Ringgit jatuh daripada kira-kira 2.42 sedolar AS pada April 1997 kepada 4.88 pada Januari 1998. Indeks KLSE jatuh daripada lebih 1,300 kepada 262.7. Kawalan modal diperkenalkan pada September 1998.9

Wait: 159 months from the 1994 high.Tempoh menunggu: 159 bulan dari puncak 1994.

RESET

Malaysia, 2014 to 15Malaysia, 2014 hingga 15

Oil fell below US$60 while the budget assumed US$105, and oil and gas were about 35% of government revenue. Foreign investors sold ringgit assets, the ringgit passed 4 per dollar for the first time since 1998, and 1MDB hurt confidence.11 KLCI was down more than 20% from its 2014 high by August 2015.12

Minyak jatuh di bawah AS$60 sedangkan belanjawan menganggap AS$105, dan minyak serta gas ialah kira-kira 35% hasil kerajaan. Pelabur asing menjual aset ringgit, ringgit melepasi 4 sedolar buat kali pertama sejak 1998, dan 1MDB menjejaskan keyakinan.11 KLCI jatuh lebih 20% dari puncak 2014 menjelang Ogos 2015.12

Still below the 2014 peak after 148 months. It passed its 2018 peak in January 2026.13Masih di bawah puncak 2014 selepas 148 bulan. Ia melepasi puncak 2018 pada Januari 2026.13

RESET

Hong Kong, 2007 to 08Hong Kong, 2007 hingga 08

The index lost 67% in about 18 months.14 It started expensive: a forward P/E of 21.9 at the 2007 peak.15 This was the global bank crisis, which reached Hong Kong through trade, credit and foreign money.

Indeks hilang 67% dalam kira-kira 18 bulan.14 Ia bermula dalam keadaan mahal: P/E ke hadapan 21.9 pada puncak 2007.15 Ini krisis bank global, yang sampai ke Hong Kong melalui perdagangan, kredit dan wang asing.

Wait: about 122 months.Tempoh menunggu: kira-kira 122 bulan.

RESET

Hong Kong, 2021 to 22Hong Kong, 2021 hingga 22

The index fell from 30,645 in February 2021 to 18,415 in March 2022, and closed at 16,701 on 12 October 2022.17 Bloomberg pointed to property debt fears, weak spending and the crackdown on big companies, and Covid rules were added in 2022.18 Goldman called it a systemic correction of over 40%.17 November 2022 then gained 26.6%, the best month since October 1998.17

Indeks jatuh daripada 30,645 pada Februari 2021 kepada 18,415 pada Mac 2022, dan ditutup pada 16,701 pada 12 Oktober 2022.17 Bloomberg menunjuk kepada kebimbangan hutang hartanah, perbelanjaan lemah dan tindakan keras ke atas syarikat besar, dan peraturan Covid ditambah pada 2022.18 Goldman menyebutnya pembetulan sistemik melebihi 40%.17 November 2022 kemudian naik 26.6%, bulan terbaik sejak Oktober 1998.17

A big bounce did not end it. Still below.Lantunan besar tidak menamatkannya. Masih di bawah.

RESET

United States, 1973 to 74Amerika Syarikat, 1973 hingga 74

The Arab oil embargo came in October 1973. US inflation rose from 3.4% in 1972 to 12.3% in 1974.21 The S&P 500 lost 48.2% between 11 January 1973 and 3 October 1974.4 Oil became inflation, and inflation became higher rates.

Embargo minyak Arab bermula pada Oktober 1973. Inflasi AS naik daripada 3.4% pada 1972 kepada 12.3% pada 1974.21 S&P 500 hilang 48.2% antara 11 Januari 1973 dan 3 Oktober 1974.4 Minyak menjadi inflasi, dan inflasi menjadi kadar faedah lebih tinggi.

Wait: 90 months.Tempoh menunggu: 90 bulan.

RESET

United States, 2000 and 2007Amerika Syarikat, 2000 dan 2007

The dot-com fall was 49.1% over 929 days. The bank crisis took 56.8% from 9 October 2007 to 9 March 2009.4 One was a valuation reset, the other a balance sheet reset.

Kejatuhan dot-com ialah 49.1% sepanjang 929 hari. Krisis bank mengambil 56.8% dari 9 Oktober 2007 hingga 9 Mac 2009.4 Satu ialah tetapan semula penilaian, satu lagi tetapan semula kunci kira-kira.

Wait: 81 and 65 months (my counts).Tempoh menunggu: 81 dan 65 bulan (kiraan saya).

SHOCK

Covid, Aug 2024, Iran warCovid, Ogos 2024, perang Iran

Covid: the S&P 500 regained its February 2020 peak in 126 trading days.4 5 Aug 2024: the Nikkei fell 12.4%, its worst day since 1987, after a BOJ hike and a jump in the yen unwound carry trades. It bottomed at 31,458 and was back at 38,063 in about two weeks.19 Iran war 2026: Hormuz closed in early March and oil went above US$100. The Nikkei lost almost 14% in March, then set a record close on 24 April.20

Covid: S&P 500 mencapai semula puncak Februari 2020 dalam 126 hari dagangan.4 5 Ogos 2024: Nikkei jatuh 12.4%, hari terburuk sejak 1987, selepas kenaikan kadar BOJ dan lonjakan yen membongkar dagangan bawa (carry trade). Ia mencecah 31,458 dan kembali ke 38,063 dalam kira-kira dua minggu.19 Perang Iran 2026: Hormuz ditutup pada awal Mac dan minyak melepasi AS$100. Nikkei hilang hampir 14% pada Mac, kemudian mencatat penutup rekod pada 24 April.20

Wait: weeks to months.Tempoh menunggu: minggu hingga bulan.

Same oil, different resultMinyak yang sama, kesan berbeza

Oil was the trigger in 1973 and again in 2026. In 1973 it fed inflation and higher rates, and stocks waited 90 months. In 2026 Japan buys 80 to 90% of its oil from the Gulf,20 yet the Nikkei was at a record within two months of the March low. What decides it is whether oil passes into prices and interest rates. That is why the November 2026 news matters: hot US inflation, talk of Fed hikes, and US bond yields near 4.99% have weighed on Hong Kong.17 I read this as a warning sign to watch, not a verdict.

Minyak menjadi pencetus pada 1973 dan sekali lagi pada 2026. Pada 1973 ia menyuap inflasi dan kadar faedah lebih tinggi, dan saham menunggu 90 bulan. Pada 2026 Jepun membeli 80 hingga 90% minyaknya dari Teluk,20 namun Nikkei mencecah rekod dalam masa dua bulan selepas paras terendah Mac. Yang menentukan ialah sama ada minyak merebak ke harga barang dan kadar faedah. Sebab itu berita November 2026 penting: inflasi AS yang panas, bualan kenaikan kadar Fed, dan hasil bon AS hampir 4.99% telah menekan Hong Kong.17 Saya baca ini sebagai tanda amaran untuk diperhatikan, bukan keputusan akhir.

04Seven hypotheses, and how each held upTujuh hipotesis, dan sejauh mana setiap satu bertahan

I wrote these down after reading the history, so treat them as readings of the evidence, not predictions.

Saya tulis hipotesis ini selepas membaca sejarahnya, jadi anggaplah ia tafsiran bukti, bukan ramalan.

H1. The type of trigger predicts recovery time better than geography.H1. Jenis pencetus meramal tempoh pulih lebih baik daripada geografi.

TestSort the episodes in Exhibit 2 by Reset or Shock. ResultSupported, with a caveat. Shocks healed in months, resets in 65 to 410. But I sorted after seeing the answer, and 1987 (a mood and positioning crash) still took 23 months.
UjianAsingkan episod dalam Pameran 2 mengikut Reset atau Shock. KeputusanDisokong, dengan satu kaveat. Kejutan pulih dalam beberapa bulan, tetapan semula dalam 65 hingga 410. Tetapi saya mengasingkan selepas tahu jawapannya, dan 1987 (kejatuhan mood dan kedudukan) masih mengambil 23 bulan.

H2. A cheap start means a short recovery.H2. Permulaan yang murah bermakna pemulihan yang singkat.

TestCompare starting valuation with the wait. ResultOnly partly. Hong Kong started 2007 at a forward P/E of 21.9 and took about 122 months. But it started 2018 at 13.4, a much lower level,15 and is still below that peak after 106 months.
UjianBandingkan penilaian permulaan dengan tempoh menunggu. KeputusanHanya sebahagian. Hong Kong bermula 2007 pada P/E ke hadapan 21.9 dan mengambil kira-kira 122 bulan. Tetapi ia bermula 2018 pada 13.4, jauh lebih rendah,15 dan masih di bawah puncak itu selepas 106 bulan.

H3. Damage to banks and borrowers gives the longest waits.H3. Kerosakan pada bank dan peminjam memberi tempoh menunggu paling lama.

TestLook at which falls came with bank losses. ResultSupported. Japan (¥100 trillion written off), 1997 Asia, and the 2007 US bank crisis are all on the long side. The 2007 case at 65 months is the shortest of them, which fits the fast US response, though I did not test that.
UjianLihat kejatuhan mana yang disertai kerugian bank. KeputusanDisokong. Jepun (¥100 trilion dihapus kira), Asia 1997 dan krisis bank AS 2007 semuanya di sebelah yang panjang. Kes 2007 pada 65 bulan ialah yang terpendek antaranya, selari dengan tindak balas pantas AS, walaupun saya tidak menguji perkara itu.

H4. For Malaysia the currency and foreign flows matter most. For Hong Kong 2021 to 22 it was new rules.H4. Bagi Malaysia, mata wang dan aliran wang asing paling penting. Bagi Hong Kong 2021 hingga 22, ia peraturan baharu.

TestCheck what moved before the index fell. ResultSupported by the record. The ringgit broke in both Malaysian falls, in 1997 and again in 2014 to 15. In Hong Kong in 2021, the crackdown on big companies came first. This is a reading of the sequence, not a statistical test.
UjianSemak apa yang bergerak sebelum indeks jatuh. KeputusanDisokong oleh rekod. Ringgit tertekan dalam kedua-dua kejatuhan Malaysia, pada 1997 dan sekali lagi pada 2014 hingga 15. Di Hong Kong pada 2021, tindakan keras ke atas syarikat besar datang dahulu. Ini bacaan urutan peristiwa, bukan ujian statistik.

H5. War hurts stocks through oil, then inflation, then rates.H5. Perang menjejaskan saham melalui minyak, kemudian inflasi, kemudian kadar faedah.

TestCompare 1973 with March 2026. ResultFits both cases. In 1973 the chain completed and the wait was 90 months. In 2026 the Nikkei recovered fast so far. The chain has not yet been shown to complete, so this one is open until the inflation data settles.
UjianBandingkan 1973 dengan Mac 2026. KeputusanSepadan dengan kedua-dua kes. Pada 1973 rantaian itu lengkap dan tempoh menunggu 90 bulan. Pada 2026 Nikkei pulih pantas setakat ini. Rantaian itu belum terbukti lengkap, jadi hipotesis ini masih terbuka sehingga data inflasi jelas.

H6. Dividends shorten recovery a lot.H6. Dividen memendekkan pemulihan dengan ketara.

TestNone. ResultNot tested. Everything in this piece is price only, and I have no dividend figures. Total return recovery is probably shorter. I cannot say by how much.
UjianTiada. KeputusanTidak diuji. Semua dalam artikel ini ialah harga sahaja, dan saya tiada angka dividen. Pemulihan jumlah pulangan mungkin lebih pendek. Saya tidak dapat kata berapa banyak.

H7. A price line cannot tell a shock from a reset.H7. Garis harga tidak dapat membezakan kejutan daripada tetapan semula.

TestRun the 200-month average rule on four markets. ResultSupported. Details in the next section.
UjianJalankan peraturan purata 200 bulan pada empat pasaran. KeputusanDisokong. Butiran di bahagian seterusnya.

What I foundApa yang saya jumpa

The win rate lies. The rule won 34 of 36 closed trades. Look at the Hang Seng trade that began in February 2022 at 22,713 and ended in February 2025 at 22,941. It booked +0.8% after 36 months. Inside it the index fell below 15,000 in October 2022, about a third under the entry. The 35.4% drawdown is that fall, and the closed-trade list does not show it.

Kadar menang berbohong. Peraturan itu menang 34 daripada 36 dagangan ditutup. Lihat dagangan Hang Seng yang bermula Februari 2022 pada 22,713 dan tamat Februari 2025 pada 22,941. Ia mencatat +0.8% selepas 36 bulan. Di dalamnya indeks jatuh di bawah 15,000 pada Oktober 2022, kira-kira sepertiga di bawah harga masuk. Susut nilai 35.4% ialah kejatuhan itu, dan senarai dagangan ditutup tidak menunjukkannya.

Resets show up as time, not as losses. On the S&P series the trade from September 1931 held 49 months and booked +28.1%, with a 54.7% drawdown on the account. The one from January 1939 held 71 months and booked +7.75%. On the Nikkei, the April 2000 trade held 80 months and booked -4.35%, and the November 2007 trade held 65 months at -11.78%. A long hold is the sign you bought a reset.

Tetapan semula muncul sebagai masa, bukan kerugian. Pada siri S&P, dagangan Sep 1931 dipegang 49 bulan dan mencatat +28.1%, dengan susut nilai akaun 54.7%. Dagangan Jan 1939 dipegang 71 bulan dan mencatat +7.75%. Pada Nikkei, dagangan April 2000 dipegang 80 bulan dan mencatat -4.35%, manakala dagangan November 2007 dipegang 65 bulan pada -11.78%. Pegangan panjang ialah tanda anda membeli sebuah tetapan semula.

The line cannot tell a shock from a reset. In March 2020 only the KLCI was bought, at 1,350.89, and it made +8.8% in two months. In April 2000 the rule bought the Nikkei at 17,973 and waited 80 months. The signal was the same, and the cause was different. That is H7.

Garis itu tidak dapat membezakan kejutan daripada tetapan semula. Pada Mac 2020 hanya KLCI dibeli, pada 1,350.89, dan ia untung +8.8% dalam dua bulan. Pada April 2000 peraturan itu membeli Nikkei pada 17,973 dan menunggu 80 bulan. Isyaratnya sama, puncanya berbeza. Itulah H7.

It trades rarely and buys deep. Hang Seng had only five trades since 2009 and the Nikkei none since April 2013, so it missed the 2020 dip there. On KLCI it bought near the bottom of the 1997 to 98 collapse, at 302.91 in August 1998, about 15% above the low of 262.7 (my calculation). That one trade made +96.3%, so a big part of the KLCI's +393% comes from a single entry.

Ia jarang berdagang dan membeli pada paras dalam. Hang Seng hanya ada lima dagangan sejak 2009 dan Nikkei tiada sejak April 2013, jadi ia terlepas penurunan 2020 di situ. Pada KLCI ia membeli hampir dasar kejatuhan 1997 hingga 98, pada 302.91 dalam Ogos 1998, kira-kira 15% di atas paras terendah 262.7 (kiraan saya). Satu dagangan itu untung +96.3%, jadi sebahagian besar +393% KLCI datang daripada satu kemasukan.

Open now. In November 2026 the rule is long on two of the four. The Hang Seng trade began in October 2026 at 23,294 and is about 4% down. The KLCI trade began in October 2026 at 1,615.60 and is also about 4% down. I record this as a fact about the test, not as a signal.

Sedang terbuka. Pada November 2026 peraturan itu sedang beli pada dua daripada empat. Dagangan Hang Seng bermula Oktober 2026 pada 23,294 dan kira-kira 4% rugi. Dagangan KLCI bermula Oktober 2026 pada 1,615.60 dan juga kira-kira 4% rugi. Saya catat ini sebagai fakta tentang ujian, bukan isyarat.

For the expertsUntuk yang arif

On a rising average, buying below it and selling above it makes almost every closed trade a winner, because the exit level is above the entry level by construction. That is why the win rate flatters. A falling average breaks it, which is how the Nikkei lost two of its five trades. So judge the rule by maximum drawdown and time underwater, not by win rate. I did not compare with buy-and-hold: the Strategy Tester's own buy-and-hold figures on some of these series did not match index levels (the S&P showed over 25 million, the Nikkei a small loss), so I dropped them.

Pada purata yang menaik, membeli di bawahnya dan menjual di atasnya menjadikan hampir setiap dagangan tutup satu kemenangan, kerana paras keluar lebih tinggi daripada paras masuk mengikut binaan. Itu sebab kadar menang memperdayakan. Purata yang menurun memecahkannya, dan begitulah Nikkei rugi dua daripada lima dagangan. Jadi nilai peraturan itu dengan susut nilai maksimum dan tempoh di bawah air, bukan kadar menang. Saya tidak membandingkan dengan beli-dan-pegang: angka beli-dan-pegang dalam Strategy Tester bagi sebahagian siri ini tidak sepadan dengan paras indeks (S&P menunjukkan lebih 25 juta, Nikkei kerugian kecil), jadi saya gugurkannya.

Limits of this testHad ujian ini
  • Price only. No dividends. Results include a 0.1% commission per trade.
  • Only 4 to 15 closed trades per market, nothing optimised or tested out of sample.
  • Start dates differ (S&P 1894, Nikkei 1995, KLCI 1998, Hang Seng 2009), so the markets are not comparable. The S&P series is monthly averages, not closes.
  • One trade can drive a market's result, as the 1998 KLCI entry does.
  • I assumed the Pine defaults (entry on a monthly close below the average, exit on a close above, no stop). I read which trade holds each drawdown from dates and prices, not from the tester.
  • An earlier run of mine used a 50-month average by mistake and was discarded. All numbers here are the 200-month rerun.
  • Harga sahaja. Tiada dividen. Keputusan termasuk komisen 0.1% setiap dagangan.
  • Hanya 4 hingga 15 dagangan ditutup setiap pasaran, tiada yang dioptimumkan atau diuji luar sampel.
  • Tarikh mula berbeza (S&P 1894, Nikkei 1995, KLCI 1998, Hang Seng 2009), jadi pasaran tidak boleh dibandingkan. Siri S&P ialah purata bulanan, bukan harga penutup.
  • Satu dagangan boleh menentukan keputusan sesuatu pasaran, seperti kemasukan KLCI pada 1998.
  • Saya menganggap tetapan asal Pine (masuk pada penutup bulanan di bawah purata, keluar pada penutup di atas, tiada henti rugi). Saya baca dagangan mana yang memegang setiap susut nilai daripada tarikh dan harga, bukan daripada tester.
  • Satu larian awal saya menggunakan purata 50 bulan secara silap dan telah dibuang. Semua angka di sini ialah larian semula 200 bulan.

06Four questions to ask while it is happeningEmpat soalan untuk ditanya semasa ia berlaku

You cannot know the cause at the moment of the fall. You can ask four plain questions. Treat the answer as a working hypothesis, never as a call on the bottom.

Anda tidak dapat tahu puncanya ketika jatuh sedang berlaku. Anda boleh tanya empat soalan mudah. Anggap jawapannya hipotesis kerja, bukan ramalan paras terendah.

  1. Are banks or borrowers in trouble?
  2. Is the central bank raising rates, or inflation rising, while stocks fall?

Keempat-empatnya tidak: mungkin kejutan kedudukan, jadi fikir dalam bulan. Mana-mana ya: fikir dalam tahun. Perkara untuk diawasi hari ini ialah soalan 2. Saya tidak dapat menjawabnya untuk anda, kerana data inflasi dan Fed masih bergerak.

08What to do with thisApa yang boleh dibuat

The following are my own analysis, not findings from the sources.

Yang berikut ialah analisis saya sendiri, bukan dapatan daripada sumber.

1

Match the money to the clock. Money needed within three years should not sit in shares, because the history above says a 20% fall can take longer than that to recover.

Padankan wang dengan jam. Wang yang diperlukan dalam tiga tahun tidak patut berada dalam saham, kerana sejarah di atas menunjukkan kejatuhan 20% boleh mengambil masa lebih lama daripada itu.

Measure: share of near-term spending held outside shares.Ukuran: bahagian perbelanjaan terdekat yang disimpan di luar saham.

2

Stage the buying. Since nobody knows if a fall is a shock or a reset, split purchases over several months instead of one entry.

Beli secara berperingkat. Memandangkan tiada siapa tahu jika kejatuhan itu kejutan atau tetapan semula, pecahkan pembelian kepada beberapa bulan, bukan satu kali masuk.

Measure: number of tranches and the gap between them.Ukuran: bilangan tranche dan jarak antaranya.

3

Write the signals down before you buy. List the four questions and what answer would make you stop adding. Decide while calm.

Tulis isyarat sebelum membeli. Senaraikan empat soalan dan jawapan yang membuat anda berhenti menambah. Putuskan ketika tenang.

Measure: a written note with dates.Ukuran: nota bertulis berserta tarikh.

4

If you are drawing down, the order of returns matters. A retiree who sells during a long wait locks in the loss. Keep enough outside shares to avoid selling at the low.

Jika anda sedang mengeluarkan wang, urutan pulangan penting. Pesara yang menjual semasa tempoh menunggu yang panjang mengunci kerugian. Simpan cukup di luar saham supaya tidak menjual pada paras rendah.

Measure: years of withdrawals held in cash or bonds.Ukuran: tahun pengeluaran yang disimpan dalam tunai atau bon.

The strongest case against my viewHujah terkuat menentang pandangan saya
  • Survivorship. The US recovered every time. Nikkei, KLCI and Hang Seng look more like what can happen when it does not go quickly.
  • Price is not wealth. A price recovery says nothing on inflation or currency.
  • Sorting after the fact. It is easy to call a fall a reset once you know it took years.
  • The next reset will not announce itself. The four questions may be answered "no" until it is too late.
  • Bias penyintas. AS pulih setiap kali. Nikkei, KLCI dan Hang Seng lebih menyerupai apa yang boleh berlaku apabila ia tidak pantas.
  • Harga bukan kekayaan. Pulih dari segi harga tidak menjawab soal inflasi atau mata wang.
  • Mengasing selepas kejadian. Mudah menamakan sesuatu tetapan semula setelah tahu ia mengambil bertahun-tahun.
  • Tetapan semula seterusnya tidak akan mengumumkan dirinya. Empat soalan itu mungkin dijawab "tidak" sehingga terlambat.
I could be wrong ifSaya mungkin silap jika
  • Total return (with dividends) shows no real difference between slow and fast markets.
  • A longer or out-of-sample test shows the Reset versus Shock split does not hold.
  • A fall I would call a shock takes more than 36 months.
  • Jumlah pulangan (dengan dividen) tidak menunjukkan beza sebenar antara pasaran perlahan dan pantas.
  • Ujian lebih panjang atau luar sampel menunjukkan pembahagian Reset lawan Shock tidak kekal.
  • Kejatuhan yang saya panggil kejutan mengambil lebih 36 bulan.

My takePandangan saya

Do not ask "how long does a correction last?" Ask "what is this fall about?" The 9.6 months is fine for a mood swing and misleading for a reset. I would plan my own money in years, and be pleasantly surprised if it is months.

Jangan tanya "berapa lama pembetulan berlangsung?" Tanya "apa sebenarnya kejatuhan ini?" Angka 9.6 bulan sesuai untuk perubahan mood, dan mengelirukan untuk tetapan semula. Saya akan merancang wang saya sendiri dalam tahun, dan bersyukur jika ia hanya beberapa bulan.
