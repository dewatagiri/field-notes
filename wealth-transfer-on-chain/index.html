Wealth Moves in a Crisis. Is Wall Street Moving On-Chain?

My hypothesis: the next big wealth transfer is being set up now, and crypto may be one place it lands. I ran the numbers myself. Some parts hold. Some do not.

Azman Taufik · 23 November 2026 · 12 min read
Comment on LinkedIn
The short version
  • Stock wealth sits at the top. The richest 10% of US households own about 87% of corporate equities (Fed, Q1 2026). In a 1926–2025 study, just 46 firms created half of the $91 trillion of net stock wealth.
  • Crises widen the gap through what people own. In 2008 households lost about the same share of wealth, but the top 10% recovered sooner because they held more kinds of assets.
  • The plumbing is moving on-chain. DTCC's tokenization service is live with 34 securities worth about $30 trillion.
  • Moving on-chain is not the same as owning a coin. The network DTCC uses is Canton, and Canton Coin holders have no claim on its fees.
  • The prices: Bitcoin is about 51% below its high and needs +104% to recover. The S&P 500 is about 6% below. ETH, SOL, LINK and ONDO are 63–84% below, so the coins linked to tokenisation have fallen more than Bitcoin.
  • I am not claiming that a crash was arranged, or that crypto is already “law” as commodities. This is my hypothesis, not a forecast.

My working idea is simple. Wealth does not shift in bull markets. It shifts in crises, and the winners are people who hold the right assets when prices are low and can hold on. What to hold is the part I wanted to test, so I went to the data.

01Who owns the stocks

You will often see 88% quoted as the share of wealth the top 10% hold in stocks. The closer figure measures something else: the share of all US corporate equities the top 10% own. The Fed puts it at 87.4% in Q1 2026, and one calculation on Q2 data gives 88.1%.

Inside the stock market itself, the winners are even fewer. Hendrik Bessembinder followed 29,754 US stocks from 1926 to 2025. Half of the $91 trillion of net wealth came from 46 firms. That is a century of data, not the 2008 crisis, so the 46 firms should not be pinned on 2008.

The 29,754 figure is the full sample. The 59.1% and the median use 29,081 firms with enough data. The −6.9% and 59.1% figures are as reported from the paper's abstract.

02What really happened in 2008

Wealth shifts in chaos, and research on the 2008 crisis partly agrees. A study from the Institute for New Economic Thinking found that all households lost about the same percentage of wealth. The top 10% recovered sooner because they owned many kinds of assets. The bottom 80% had more of their wealth in housing, and many have not recovered.

The Federal Reserve's own note says the same by 2016. The top 10% had more wealth than in 2007. The bottom 90% were still below 2007 levels.

What this supports

Owning assets that recover is what separated people after 2008. The gap opened through what they held and whether they could hold on. It did not come from a rule that stocks always bounce first.

03Is this a crash?

It feels like a massive fall. For crypto, it is. For stocks, not yet at that scale. The S&P 500 fell as much as 7.1% from its 6 October high. The Nasdaq was 11.9% below its high on 5 November. In 2008 the stock market fell by nearly half.

You will also hear that shadow banks are crashing the market on purpose to push everyone into crypto. I found no evidence for it. It may be unprovable either way, so I leave it out of the argument. The AI-stock sell-off has its own stated causes: the cost of borrowing for data centres, and worries that prices ran too high.

04The strongest part: Wall Street's plumbing is moving

Here the evidence is firm. DTCC, the clearing house for US securities, switched on a tokenization service. It covers 34 securities, about $30 trillion in market value, and runs on the Canton Network and DTCC's own private Besu blockchain. The SEC gave DTCC a no-action letter in December 2025 for three years, covering the Russell 1000, ETFs and Treasuries. More than 50 firms joined the industry working group, including BlackRock, Circle, Nasdaq and NYSE.

$4.7 quad.DTCC transactions processed in 2025 (a flow, not assets)
$114Tassets DTCC holds in custody
34securities live in tokenized form

The $4.7 quadrillion figure that circulates is not the size of the market. It is the value of transactions processed in a year. Assets held are about $114 trillion. The launch date is also quoted as 23 October. The report I found is dated 28 October and says Wednesday, so I leave the exact day open.

05Using a blockchain is not owning its coin

This is where the investment case has to be careful. Canton Coin's fees are burned, and holders have no governance, fee or revenue rights, according to DefiLlama (updated 23 June 2026). The SEC staff said in January 2026 that what a crypto asset gives its holder depends on the model. In one issuer-sponsored model, the crypto asset conveys none of the security's rights. That is staff opinion, not a rule.

The trap

“Wall Street uses chain X” and “holders of coin X get paid” are two different claims. The first is now documented for DTCC and Canton. The second is not.

06Is it already law?

Some say certain coins are now law as commodities. Not yet. The CLARITY Act, which would split oversight between the CFTC and the SEC, failed to advance in the Senate on 15 September 2026, and passage in this Congress is widely seen as unlikely. What exists today is a joint SEC-CFTC interpretation from March 2026. It is agency policy and can be revoked by a vote.

07Where real money has gone so far

If the thesis is right, buying should be visible. In early November 2026, US spot ETFs had taken in about $55.8 billion for Bitcoin and $12.0 billion for Ethereum, against $1.4 billion for Solana and $0.3 billion for Hyperliquid. Bitcoin is about 80% of the four (my calculation). This is a tracker snapshot I could not open directly, so please re-check it.

Access is not demand. A Chainlink ETF held $87.5 million when a second one launched, and a VanEck AVAX ETF had zero net inflows on day one. The test is money that stays, not products that launch.

08How far have prices fallen?

I measured each asset from its own record high. Bitcoin is about 51% below its high. The S&P 500 is about 6% below. Most of the coins that are said to benefit from Wall Street's move are further down than Bitcoin, not less.

The pain is in the recovery maths. A fall of X% needs a gain of 1/(1−X) − 1 to get back.

+7%S&P 500 to recover
+104%Bitcoin to recover
+171%Ethereum to recover
+1,136%Dogecoin to recover

Two results from this table matter for the thesis. The tokenisation-linked coins (ETH, SOL, LINK, ONDO, BNB, HBAR) did not fall less than Bitcoin. They fell more. And the stock sell-off is concentrated: NVDA is down 37% while the index is down 6%.

What would ONDO need to be worth its price? (illustration)

BlackRock has no coin of its own. Its BUIDL fund token is run through Securitize on several chains. ONDO is the token of a separate company, Ondo Finance. My assumptions: price $0.349, total supply 10 billion (please check), so fully diluted value about $3.5 billion. If the market paid 20 times annual revenue, revenue would need to be about $174 million.

  • At a 0.10% fee, that needs about $174 billion of assets.
  • At a 0.25% fee, about $70 billion.
  • At a 0.50% fee, about $35 billion.

BUIDL, the largest tokenised fund in my sources, holds about $2.3 to $2.8 billion. The fee rates are my assumptions, not Ondo's published rates. And even then, DefiLlama shows ONDO holders have no claim on the equity or the revenue.

09The case for the thesis, and the case against

For

Ownership decides who recovers

After 2008 the gap grew through assets held. A prepared holder buys when prices are low.

For

The infrastructure shift is real

DTCC, the SEC letter, Nasdaq and NYSE support, and a $30 trillion eligible pool are documented.

Against

Buying power is narrow

Bitcoin takes about 80% of tracked ETF inflows. Most other tokens show no such channel.

Against

The token may not capture the value

Canton shows a chain can be used by Wall Street while its coin holders earn no fee.

10Four checks before calling any coin a “wealth transfer coin”

Check 1

Is anyone buying the coin?

Look for ETF flows, filings or balance sheets. A pilot is not a purchase.

Check 2

Does usage pay the holder?

Check fees, revenue and token holder revenue on DefiLlama. Incentives paid in new tokens are a cost.

Check 3

Is the legal status real?

Separate law from staff statements and agency interpretations that can be revoked.

Check 4

Can you hold through a fall of 50% or more?

A 50% loss needs +100% to recover. An 80% loss needs +400%. Size your position so you are not forced to sell.

My hypothesis

This is my own thinking. It is a hypothesis, not a forecast and not a tested system. I may be wrong. Read it, test it yourself, and take it at your own risk.

Here is how I read it.

  • A crisis widens the gap through what people hold, and whether they can hold on. That is what the 2008 data shows.
  • Wall Street is moving its plumbing on-chain. The first money in that move goes to whoever owns the rails and issues the assets. It does not flow to coin holders by default.
  • Bitcoin has a real buyer base through ETFs and has fallen about half. Most other coins have fallen 60–90% with no claim on the fees. They have to prove themselves before I treat them as part of this story.
  • So I would rather hold cash to buy at low prices, and things that own the fee, than a list of coins that only share a theme.
Over to you

If Wall Street settles on-chain, who do you think earns the fee?

Kekayaan Berpindah Ketika Krisis. Adakah Wall Street Sedang Beralih ke Blockchain?

Hipotesis saya: pemindahan kekayaan besar yang seterusnya sedang disediakan sekarang, dan kripto mungkin salah satu tempat ia mendarat. Saya jalankan sendiri angkanya. Ada bahagian yang kukuh. Ada yang tidak.

Azman Taufik · 23 November 2026 · 12 minit bacaan
Komen di LinkedIn
Ringkasan pantas
  • Kekayaan saham berada di puncak. 10% isi rumah AS terkaya memiliki kira-kira 87% ekuiti korporat (Fed, S1 2026). Dalam kajian 1926–2025, hanya 46 firma mencipta separuh daripada AS$91 trilion kekayaan saham bersih.
  • Krisis melebarkan jurang melalui apa yang dimiliki. Pada 2008, isi rumah kehilangan peratusan kekayaan yang hampir sama, tetapi 10% teratas pulih lebih cepat kerana memiliki lebih banyak jenis aset.
  • Saluran asas pasaran sedang berpindah ke rantaian. Perkhidmatan tokenisasi DTCC sudah beroperasi dengan 34 sekuriti bernilai kira-kira AS$30 trilion.
  • Berpindah ke rantaian tidak sama dengan memiliki syiling. Salah satu rangkaian yang digunakan DTCC ialah Canton, dan pemegang Canton Coin tiada tuntutan ke atas yurannya.
  • Harga: Bitcoin kira-kira 51% di bawah paras tertinggi dan perlu naik 104% untuk pulih. S&P 500 kira-kira 6% di bawah. ETH, SOL, LINK dan ONDO berada 63–84% di bawah, jadi syiling berkaitan tokenisasi jatuh lebih daripada Bitcoin.
  • Saya tidak mendakwa bahawa kejatuhan itu diatur, atau bahawa kripto sudah “sah di sisi undang-undang” sebagai komoditi. Ini hipotesis saya, bukan ramalan.

Idea kerja saya mudah. Kekayaan tidak berpindah semasa pasaran naik. Ia berpindah semasa krisis, dan pemenangnya ialah mereka yang memegang aset yang betul ketika harga rendah dan mampu bertahan. Apa yang patut dipegang itulah yang saya mahu uji, jadi saya turun kepada data.

01Siapa yang memiliki saham

Angka 88% sering disebut sebagai bahagian kekayaan 10% teratas dalam saham. Angka yang lebih tepat mengukur perkara lain: bahagian ekuiti korporat AS yang dimiliki 10% teratas. Fed meletakkannya pada 87.4% bagi S1 2026, dan satu pengiraan atas data S2 memberi 88.1%.

Di dalam pasaran saham sendiri, pemenangnya lebih sedikit lagi. Hendrik Bessembinder menjejak 29,754 saham AS dari 1926 hingga 2025. Separuh daripada AS$91 trilion kekayaan bersih datang daripada 46 firma. Itu data seratus tahun, bukan krisis 2008, jadi 46 firma itu tidak wajar dikaitkan dengan 2008.

59.1%firma kalah kepada bil Perbendaharaan

Angka 29,754 ialah sampel penuh. Angka 59.1% dan median menggunakan 29,081 firma yang mempunyai data mencukupi. Angka −6.9% dan 59.1% seperti yang dilaporkan daripada abstrak kajian.

02Apa sebenarnya berlaku pada 2008

Kekayaan berpindah ketika huru-hara, dan kajian tentang krisis 2008 sebahagiannya bersetuju. Satu kajian Institute for New Economic Thinking mendapati semua isi rumah kehilangan peratusan kekayaan yang hampir sama. 10% teratas pulih lebih cepat kerana memiliki pelbagai jenis aset. 80% terbawah lebih banyak menyimpan kekayaan dalam rumah, dan ramai belum pulih.

Nota Federal Reserve sendiri berkata perkara yang sama menjelang 2016. 10% teratas mempunyai kekayaan lebih tinggi daripada 2007. 90% terbawah masih di bawah paras 2007.

Apa yang disokong

Memiliki aset yang pulih ialah yang membezakan orang selepas 2008. Jurang terbuka melalui apa yang mereka pegang dan sama ada mereka mampu bertahan. Ia bukan kerana peraturan bahawa saham sentiasa melantun dahulu.

03Adakah ini kejatuhan besar?

Ia terasa seperti kejatuhan besar. Bagi kripto, memang. Bagi saham, belum pada skala itu. S&P 500 jatuh sehingga 7.1% daripada puncak 6 Oktober. Nasdaq berada 11.9% di bawah puncaknya pada 5 November. Pada 2008, pasaran saham jatuh hampir separuh.

Anda juga akan dengar bahawa bank bayangan sengaja menjatuhkan pasaran untuk menolak semua orang ke kripto. Saya tidak menjumpai bukti. Ia mungkin tidak dapat dibuktikan sama ada ya atau tidak, jadi saya tidak memasukkannya dalam hujah. Jualan besar-besaran saham AI mempunyai sebab yang dinyatakan sendiri: kos pinjaman untuk pusat data, dan kebimbangan harga naik terlalu tinggi.

04Bahagian paling kukuh: saluran asas Wall Street sedang berpindah

Di sini bukti kukuh. DTCC, rumah penjelasan sekuriti AS, telah menghidupkan perkhidmatan tokenisasi. Ia meliputi 34 sekuriti, kira-kira AS$30 trilion nilai pasaran, dan berjalan di rangkaian Canton serta blockchain peribadi Besu milik DTCC sendiri. SEC memberi DTCC surat tiada tindakan pada Disember 2025 untuk tiga tahun, meliputi Russell 1000, ETF dan Perbendaharaan. Lebih 50 firma menyertai kumpulan kerja industri, termasuk BlackRock, Circle, Nasdaq dan NYSE.

Angka AS$4.7 kuadrilion yang tersebar bukan saiz pasaran. Ia ialah nilai transaksi yang diproses setahun. Aset dalam jagaan kira-kira AS$114 trilion. Tarikh pelancaran juga disebut sebagai 23 Oktober. Laporan yang saya jumpai bertarikh 28 Oktober dan menyebut Rabu, jadi saya biarkan tarikh tepat terbuka.

05Menggunakan blockchain tidak sama dengan memiliki syilingnya

Di sini kes pelaburan perlu berhati-hati. Yuran Canton Coin dibakar, dan pemegangnya tiada hak tadbir urus, yuran atau hasil, menurut DefiLlama (dikemas kini 23 Jun 2026). Kakitangan SEC berkata pada Januari 2026 bahawa apa yang diberikan sesuatu aset kripto kepada pemegangnya bergantung pada modelnya. Dalam satu model penerbit tajaan, aset kripto itu tidak membawa apa-apa hak sekuriti. Itu pandangan kakitangan, bukan peraturan.

Perangkap

“Wall Street guna rantaian X” dan “pemegang syiling X dibayar” ialah dua dakwaan berbeza. Yang pertama kini didokumenkan bagi DTCC dan Canton. Yang kedua tidak.

06Adakah ia sudah menjadi undang-undang?

Ada yang berkata sesetengah syiling kini sah sebagai komoditi. Belum lagi. Akta CLARITY, yang akan membahagikan pengawasan antara CFTC dan SEC, gagal maju di Senat pada 15 September 2026, dan kelulusan dalam Kongres ini dilihat tidak mungkin. Yang ada hari ini ialah tafsiran bersama SEC-CFTC dari Mac 2026. Ia dasar agensi dan boleh ditarik balik melalui undian.

07Ke mana wang sebenar telah pergi setakat ini

Jika tesis ini betul, pembelian sepatutnya kelihatan. Pada awal November 2026, ETF spot AS menerima kira-kira AS$55.8 bilion untuk Bitcoin dan AS$12.0 bilion untuk Ethereum, berbanding AS$1.4 bilion untuk Solana dan AS$0.3 bilion untuk Hyperliquid. Bitcoin ialah kira-kira 80% daripada keempat-empatnya (pengiraan saya). Ini paparan penjejak yang tidak dapat saya buka terus, jadi sila semak semula.

Akses bukan permintaan. ETF Chainlink memegang AS$87.5 juta apabila yang kedua dilancarkan, dan ETF AVAX VanEck tiada aliran masuk bersih pada hari pertama. Ujiannya ialah wang yang kekal, bukan produk yang dilancarkan.

08Setakat mana harga telah jatuh?

Saya ukur setiap aset daripada paras tertingginya sendiri. Bitcoin kira-kira 51% di bawah paras tertinggi. S&P 500 kira-kira 6% di bawah. Kebanyakan syiling yang dikatakan mendapat manfaat daripada peralihan Wall Street berada lebih jauh ke bawah daripada Bitcoin, bukan kurang.

Kesakitan sebenar ada pada pengiraan pemulihan. Kejatuhan X% memerlukan kenaikan 1/(1−X) − 1 untuk kembali.

Dua keputusan daripada jadual ini penting untuk tesis. Syiling berkaitan tokenisasi (ETH, SOL, LINK, ONDO, BNB, HBAR) tidak jatuh kurang daripada Bitcoin. Ia jatuh lebih. Dan jualan saham tertumpu: NVDA jatuh 37% manakala indeks jatuh 6%.

BlackRock tiada syiling sendiri. Token dana BUIDL miliknya dijalankan melalui Securitize di beberapa rantaian. ONDO ialah token syarikat lain, Ondo Finance. Andaian saya: harga $0.349, bekalan penuh 10 bilion (sila semak), jadi nilai dicairkan penuh kira-kira $3.5 bilion. Jika pasaran membayar 20 kali hasil tahunan, hasil perlu sekitar $174 juta.

09Kes yang menyokong tesis, dan yang menentang

Menyokong

Pemilikan menentukan siapa yang pulih

Selepas 2008 jurang membesar melalui aset yang dipegang. Pemegang yang bersedia membeli ketika harga rendah.

Menyokong

Peralihan infrastruktur itu benar

DTCC, surat SEC, sokongan Nasdaq dan NYSE, dan kumpulan layak AS$30 trilion telah didokumenkan.

10Empat semakan sebelum menggelar mana-mana syiling “syiling pemindahan kekayaan”

Semakan 1

Adakah sesiapa membeli syiling itu?

Cari aliran ETF, pemfailan atau kunci kira-kira. Projek perintis bukan pembelian.

Semakan 2

Adakah penggunaan membayar pemegang?

Semak yuran, hasil dan hasil pemegang token di DefiLlama. Insentif yang dibayar dengan token baharu ialah kos.

Semakan 3

Adakah status undang-undangnya benar?

Bezakan undang-undang daripada kenyataan kakitangan dan tafsiran agensi yang boleh ditarik balik.

Semakan 4

Bolehkah anda bertahan ketika jatuh 50% atau lebih?

Kerugian 50% memerlukan +100% untuk pulih. Kerugian 80% memerlukan +400%. Saiz kedudukan anda supaya tidak terpaksa menjual.

Hipotesis saya

Ini pemikiran saya sendiri. Ia hipotesis, bukan ramalan dan bukan sistem yang telah diuji. Saya mungkin salah. Baca, uji sendiri, dan ambil pada risiko anda sendiri.

Beginilah saya membacanya.

  • Krisis melebarkan jurang melalui apa yang dipegang orang, dan sama ada mereka mampu bertahan. Itulah yang ditunjukkan data 2008.
  • Wall Street sedang memindahkan saluran asasnya ke rantaian. Wang pertama dalam peralihan itu pergi kepada pemilik laluan dan penerbit aset. Ia tidak mengalir kepada pemegang syiling secara automatik.
  • Bitcoin mempunyai pangkalan pembeli sebenar melalui ETF dan telah jatuh kira-kira separuh. Kebanyakan syiling lain jatuh 60–90% tanpa tuntutan ke atas yuran. Mereka perlu membuktikan diri dahulu sebelum saya anggap sebahagian daripada cerita ini.
  • Jadi saya lebih rela memegang tunai untuk membeli pada harga rendah, dan aset yang memiliki yuran, daripada senarai syiling yang hanya berkongsi tema.
