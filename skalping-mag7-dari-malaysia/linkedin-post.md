## Bahasa Malaysia (default)

Bolehkah anda skalping saham Magnificent 7 dari Malaysia dan masih untung?
Julat harian memang cukup besar. Yang menentukan ialah kos setiap dagangan.

Contoh: beli dan jual 100 saham NVDA (kira-kira US$14,600 setiap belah) di Moomoo Malaysia berharga kira-kira US$41 selepas promosi 180 hari. Itu 28 bps, dan 20 bps daripadanya duti setem (andaian: kedua-dua belah).

Beli satu saham sahaja? Yuran tetap US$0.99 setiap pesanan menjadikan satu pusingan kira-kira US$2.50 hingga US$3.10, atau 90 hingga 175 bps.

Tiga angka (25 Nov 2026, pengiraan saya daripada halaman yuran Moomoo Malaysia dan Finviz):
- ATR 14 hari: 2.3% (AAPL) hingga 5.0% (TSLA) bagi enam daripada tujuh saham
- Kos satu pusingan: kira-kira 27 bps selepas promosi 180 hari, 20 bps daripadanya duti setem (andaian: kedua-dua belah)
- Untuk pulang modal, anda perlu mendapat 5% hingga 12% daripada julat purata sehari

Skalp yang menyasarkan 0.5% menyerahkan lebih separuh kepada kos. Leveraj tidak mengubah nisbah itu, tetapi pada 4x kos bulanan satu pusingan sehari ialah kira-kira 22% ekuiti.

Saya menggunakan kadar pasaran bagi angka yang belum diterbitkan Moomoo (sebaran tukaran mata wang, kadar margin USD, belah duti setem) dan akan minta pengesahan.

## English

Can you scalp the Magnificent 7 from Malaysia and still make money?
The daily range is big enough. The cost of each trade decides the result.

Example: buying and selling 100 shares of NVDA (about US$14,600 each way) on Moomoo Malaysia costs about US$41 after the 180-day promo. That is 28 bps, and 20 bps of it is stamp duty (assumed on both sides).

Only one share? The flat US$0.99 per order makes a round trip about US$2.50 to US$3.10, or 90 to 175 bps.

Three numbers (25 Nov 2026, my calculation from the Moomoo Malaysia fee page and Finviz):
- 14-day ATR: 2.3% (AAPL) to 5.0% (TSLA) for six of the seven
- Round-trip cost: about 27 bps after the 180-day promo, about 20 bps of it stamp duty (assumed on both sides)
- To break even you must capture 5% to 12% of an average day's range

A scalp that aims for 0.5% hands over more than half to costs. Leverage does not change that ratio, but at 4x one round trip a day costs about 22% of equity a month.

Where Moomoo has not published a figure (FX spread, USD margin rate, which sides stamp duty applies to) I used the market rate and will ask them to confirm.
