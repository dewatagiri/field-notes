If you could buy the exact low and sell the exact high of a Magnificent 7 stock every day, would costs matter? No. A 4% range pays for a 0.27% cost many times over.

Jika anda dapat membeli pada paras terendah tepat dan menjual pada paras tertinggi tepat saham Magnificent 7 setiap hari, adakah kos penting? Tidak. Julat 4% menampung kos 0.27% berkali-kali ganda.

So the better question is how much of the range an ordinary trader captures, and how much of each trade the platform keeps.

Jadi soalan yang lebih tepat ialah berapa banyak julat yang dapat diperoleh peniaga biasa, dan berapa banyak daripada setiap dagangan yang diambil platform.

I took the fees from the Moomoo Malaysia US fee page and the ranges from Finviz, both as at 25 November 2026, and added my own calculations. Where Moomoo has not published a figure, I assumed the market rate and say so. I will ask Moomoo to confirm.

Saya mengambil yuran daripada halaman yuran AS Moomoo Malaysia dan julat daripada Finviz, kedua-duanya pada 25 November 2026, kemudian menambah pengiraan sendiri. Jika Moomoo belum menerbitkan sesuatu angka, saya menganggap kadar pasaran dan menyatakannya. Saya akan meminta Moomoo mengesahkan.

03How I worked it out, step by stepCara saya mengira, langkah demi langkah

Every number in this article comes from five simple steps. Here they are for 100 shares of NVDA. You can repeat them with any stock using your own broker's fee page. 1 bps (one basis point) is 0.01%, so 27 bps is 0.27%.

Setiap angka dalam artikel ini datang daripada lima langkah mudah. Berikut langkahnya bagi 100 saham NVDA. Anda boleh mengulanginya dengan mana-mana saham menggunakan halaman yuran broker anda. 1 bps (satu mata asas) ialah 0.01%, jadi 27 bps ialah 0.27%.

1
How far does the stock move in a day?Berapa jauh saham bergerak dalam sehari?
ATR is the average daily move in dollars. Divide it by the price to get a percentage.
US$13.02 ÷ US$145.89 = 8.9% (AAPL: US$8.00 ÷ US$350.81 = 2.3%). For 100 shares, an average day moves about US$1,302 (13.02 × 100).
ATR ialah purata pergerakan harian dalam dolar. Bahagikan dengan harga untuk mendapat peratusan.
US$13.02 ÷ US$145.89 = 8.9% (AAPL: US$8.00 ÷ US$350.81 = 2.3%). Bagi 100 saham, purata sehari bergerak kira-kira US$1,302 (13.02 × 100).
2
How big is the trade?Berapa besar dagangan itu?
Shares times price. 100 × US$145.89 = US$14,589 each way.
Bilangan saham darab harga. 100 × US$145.89 = US$14,589 setiap belah.
3
What does each fee come to?Berapa setiap yuran?
Commission: 0.03% × 14,589 = US$4.38 (zero during the 180-day promo).
Platform fee: US$0.99 a fixed amount per order.
Settlement: 100 × US$0.003 = US$0.30.
Stamp duty: RM1 for every RM1,000, which is about 0.1%, so 0.1% × 14,589 = US$14.59 (my assumption that it applies on both the buy and the sell).
SEC fee, sell only: 0.0000206 × 14,589 = US$0.30.
Komisen: 0.03% × 14,589 = US$4.38 (sifar semasa promosi 180 hari).
Yuran platform: US$0.99 jumlah tetap setiap pesanan.
Penyelesaian: 100 × US$0.003 = US$0.30.
Duti setem: RM1 bagi setiap RM1,000, iaitu kira-kira 0.1%, maka 0.1% × 14,589 = US$14.59 (andaian saya ia dikenakan pada beli dan jual).
Yuran SEC, jualan sahaja: 0.0000206 × 14,589 = US$0.30.
4
Add up the buy and the sellJumlahkan beli dan jual
Buy: 4.38 + 0.99 + 0.30 + 14.59 = US$20.26.
Sell: the same, plus the SEC fee = US$20.56.
Round trip: US$40.82. As a share of the trade: 40.82 ÷ 14,589 = 0.28%, which is 28 bps.
Beli: 4.38 + 0.99 + 0.30 + 14.59 = US$20.26.
Jual: sama, ditambah yuran SEC = US$20.56.
Satu pusingan: US$40.82. Sebagai bahagian daripada dagangan: 40.82 ÷ 14,589 = 0.28%, iaitu 28 bps.
5
How much of the day's move must you win just to break even?Berapa banyak pergerakan harian yang mesti anda perolehi sekadar untuk pulang modal?
Cost divided by the day's move in dollars: 40.82 ÷ 1,302 = 3.1%. If you also lose 10 bps to slippage (US$14.59 on this trade): (40.82 + 14.59) ÷ 1,302 = 4.3%.
In words: you must catch about 3 to 4 cents of every dollar the stock moves in a day, only to get your money back.
Kos dibahagi pergerakan harian dalam dolar: 40.82 ÷ 1,302 = 3.1%. Jika anda juga kehilangan 10 bps akibat gelinciran (US$14.59 pada dagangan ini): (40.82 + 14.59) ÷ 1,302 = 4.3%.
Dalam bahasa mudah: anda mesti memperoleh kira-kira 3 hingga 4 sen bagi setiap dolar pergerakan saham sehari, hanya untuk pulang modal.
For the expertsUntuk yang pakar

Break-even capture = (round-trip fees + slippage) ÷ (ATR × shares). ATR(14) is a 14-day average of true range, which includes overnight gaps, so it overstates a regular session's high minus low. Costs here are linear in trade value except the US$0.99 per order and the minimums.

Perolehan pulang modal = (yuran satu pusingan + gelinciran) ÷ (ATR × bilangan saham). ATR(14) ialah purata 14 hari bagi julat sebenar, termasuk jurang semalaman, jadi ia melebihkan tertinggi tolak terendah sesi biasa. Kos di sini bersifat linear terhadap nilai dagangan kecuali US$0.99 setiap pesanan dan jumlah minimum.

Fractional shares are worseSaham pecahan lebih teruk

For orders under one share, the platform fee is 0.99% of the amount, capped at US$0.99, and stamp duty still applies. On a US$100 order that is about US$0.99 + US$0.22 = US$1.21 each way, so about US$2.42, or 2.4%, for the round trip. Commission, settlement and SEC fees are waived for these orders, but it does not make up for it.

Bagi pesanan kurang satu saham, yuran platform ialah 0.99% daripada jumlah, dihadkan pada US$0.99, dan duti setem masih dikenakan. Pada pesanan US$100, itu kira-kira US$0.99 + US$0.22 = US$1.21 setiap belah, jadi kira-kira US$2.42, atau 2.4%, bagi satu pusingan. Komisen, yuran penyelesaian dan SEC dihapuskan bagi pesanan ini, tetapi ia tidak menampung kos tersebut.

What this meansMaksudnya

The 27 bps in the rest of this article is the cost at 100 shares. With one share, expect 90 to 175 bps, and a break-even of 20% to 40% of an average day's move. The smaller the account, the harder the arithmetic.

Kos 27 bps dalam bahagian lain artikel ini ialah kos pada 100 saham. Dengan satu saham, jangkakan 90 hingga 175 bps, dan titik pulang modal 20% hingga 40% daripada purata pergerakan sehari. Semakin kecil akaun, semakin sukar pengiraannya.

05The cost is per trade, not per dayKos dikira bagi setiap dagangan, bukan setiap hari

Set against a full day's range, 27 bps looks small. A scalper does not take the full range. A scalper takes 0.3% to 1% per trade, and pays the 27 bps every time.

Berbanding julat sehari penuh, 27 bps nampak kecil. Pemain skalping tidak mengambil julat penuh. Mereka mengambil 0.3% hingga 1% setiap dagangan, dan membayar 27 bps setiap kali.

What the data saysApa kata angka

On six of the seven stocks you need 5% to 12% of the day's range on fees alone, and 7% to 16% with slippage. That is possible. It is also a hurdle you carry on every trade, before the market has to be right.

Bagi enam daripada tujuh saham, anda perlukan 5% hingga 12% julat sehari menggunakan yuran sahaja, dan 7% hingga 16% dengan gelinciran. Itu boleh dicapai. Namun ia halangan yang anda tanggung pada setiap dagangan, sebelum pasaran perlu menyebelahi anda.

06What leverage does to the costKesan leveraj terhadap kos

Leverage does not change the break-even share of the range, because cost scales with the position. It changes what the cost does to your equity.

Leveraj tidak mengubah bahagian julat untuk pulang modal, kerana kos berskala dengan saiz kedudukan. Ia mengubah kesan kos ke atas ekuiti anda.

Worked example (my calculation)Contoh pengiraan (pengiraan saya)
  • An example account, not a real one: equity US$5,000 at 4x gives US$20,000 of positions (5,000 × 4). A smaller account pays more, as section 04 shows.
  • One round trip at 27 bps costs about US$54 (20,000 × 0.27%), or 1.1% of equity.
  • Trading once a day for 20 days: 20 × US$54 = US$1,080 a month, or 21.6% of your US$5,000. At 1x (US$5,000 positions) the same habit costs about US$270, or 5.4%. Leverage multiplies the fees because they are charged on the position, not on your own money.
  • A 4% ATR move is 16% of equity.
  • If margin interest applies to a same-day position, US$15,000 borrowed at 6.8% a year is about US$2.80 a day. Moomoo's page gives 6.8% for MYR financing. I could not find the USD rate, and I have assumed the same figure.
  • Contoh akaun, bukan akaun sebenar: ekuiti US$5,000 pada 4x memberi kedudukan US$20,000 (5,000 × 4). Akaun lebih kecil membayar lebih, seperti ditunjukkan dalam bahagian 04.
  • Satu pusingan pada 27 bps berharga kira-kira US$54 (20,000 × 0.27%), atau 1.1% ekuiti.
  • Berdagang sekali sehari selama 20 hari: 20 × US$54 = US$1,080 sebulan, atau 21.6% daripada US$5,000 anda. Pada 1x (kedudukan US$5,000), tabiat yang sama berharga kira-kira US$270, atau 5.4%. Leveraj melipatgandakan yuran kerana yuran dikenakan pada saiz kedudukan, bukan pada wang sendiri.
  • Pergerakan ATR 4% bersamaan 16% ekuiti.
  • Jika faedah margin dikenakan pada kedudukan hari yang sama, pinjaman US$15,000 pada 6.8% setahun ialah kira-kira US$2.80 sehari. Halaman Moomoo memberi 6.8% bagi pembiayaan MYR. Saya tidak menemui kadar USD dan menganggap angka yang sama.

07A prop firm alternative: BitfundedAlternatif firma prop: Bitfunded

Some traders look at prop firms such as Bitfunded instead, because the fee per trade is lower. I read Bitfunded's own FAQ, terms and user guide on 25 Nov 2026. It is a different product, so the comparison needs care.

Sesetengah peniaga melihat firma prop seperti Bitfunded kerana yuran setiap dagangan lebih rendah. Saya membaca FAQ, terma dan panduan pengguna Bitfunded sendiri pada 25 Nov 2026. Ia produk yang berbeza, jadi perbandingan perlu berhati-hati.

What Bitfunded says about itselfApa kata Bitfunded tentang dirinya
  • It is a simulated environment on live market data. You trade simulated capital, you cannot lose more than the entry fee, and the profit share it pays out is real USDT.
  • Trading fee: 0.04% of position size to open and 0.04% to close.
  • Leverage: 1x to 5x. Daily loss limit 3% to 5% and maximum loss 3% to 10% of the starting balance, depending on the challenge type. Floating losses count.
  • Entry fee: from US$79 (2-Step, US$5,000 account) or US$99 (1-Step, US$5,000). The fee is refunded only for the 2-Step, after both stages are passed and the first profit split day is reached.
  • Profit split: 80% (60% on Instant Funding). No hedging, no bots, at most 5 open positions, and traditional pairs must be closed within 5 days.
  • Ia persekitaran simulasi menggunakan data pasaran langsung. Anda berdagang dengan modal simulasi, tidak boleh rugi melebihi yuran penyertaan, dan perkongsian untung yang dibayar ialah USDT sebenar.
  • Yuran dagangan: 0.04% daripada saiz kedudukan untuk membuka dan 0.04% untuk menutup.
  • Leveraj: 1x hingga 5x. Had kerugian harian 3% hingga 5% dan kerugian maksimum 3% hingga 10% daripada baki permulaan, bergantung pada jenis cabaran. Kerugian terapung dikira.
  • Yuran penyertaan: bermula US$79 (2 Langkah, akaun US$5,000) atau US$99 (1 Langkah, US$5,000). Yuran dipulangkan hanya bagi 2 Langkah, selepas kedua-dua peringkat lulus dan hari pembahagian untung pertama tercapai.
  • Pembahagian untung: 80% (60% bagi Instant Funding). Tiada lindung nilai, tiada bot, paling banyak 5 kedudukan terbuka, dan pasangan tradisional mesti ditutup dalam 5 hari.

Why it is not a like-for-like comparisonMengapa ia bukan perbandingan setara
  • It is not your money in the market. You pay an entry fee, and what you keep depends on passing the rules. The pages show no pass rate, so I cannot say whether the entry fee pays off.
  • The loss limits bite. At 5x, a stock with a 4% ATR moves 20% of your account on an average day, against a daily limit of 4% to 5%. Holding through a normal day only fits about 1x.
  • Nasdaq: the guide names XAU, XAG and TSLA as traditional pairs, and the FAQ says there are 20 (the home page says 25 or more). TSLA is listed on Nasdaq. I found no Nasdaq index, and the markets page loads empty. To be confirmed on the platform.
  • Not published on the pages I read: how the simulated price and spread are set, and whether the 0.04% applies to traditional pairs. I assumed it does.
  • Ia bukan wang anda di pasaran. Anda membayar yuran penyertaan, dan apa yang anda simpan bergantung pada lulus peraturan. Halaman itu tidak menunjukkan kadar lulus, jadi saya tidak dapat menyatakan sama ada yuran penyertaan berbaloi.
  • Had kerugian sangat ketat. Pada 5x, saham dengan ATR 4% menggerakkan 20% akaun anda pada hari biasa, berbanding had harian 4% hingga 5%. Memegang sepanjang hari biasa hanya sesuai pada kira-kira 1x.
  • Nasdaq: panduan menamakan XAU, XAG dan TSLA sebagai pasangan tradisional, dan FAQ menyatakan terdapat 20 (laman utama menyatakan 25 atau lebih). TSLA disenaraikan di Nasdaq. Saya tidak menemui indeks Nasdaq, dan halaman pasaran dimuatkan kosong. Menunggu pengesahan pada platform.
  • Tidak diterbitkan pada halaman yang saya baca: bagaimana harga dan sebaran simulasi ditetapkan, dan sama ada 0.04% terpakai pada pasangan tradisional. Saya menganggap ia terpakai.
What this meansMaksudnya

Per trade, Bitfunded costs about a third of Moomoo at 100 shares and far less at one share. You swap a trading cost for an entry fee and strict loss limits. For a small account that is a real trade-off, and it deserves its own test before anyone pays.

Bagi setiap dagangan, Bitfunded berharga kira-kira satu pertiga daripada Moomoo pada 100 saham dan jauh lebih rendah pada satu saham. Anda menukar kos dagangan dengan yuran penyertaan dan had kerugian yang ketat. Bagi akaun kecil, itu pertukaran sebenar, dan ia wajar diuji sendiri sebelum sesiapa membayar.

08The case against my own argumentHujah menentang hujah saya sendiri

A fair critic would say the following, and some of it is right.

Pengkritik yang adil akan berkata yang berikut, dan sebahagiannya betul.

  • If stamp duty applies on one side only, the round trip falls to about 17 bps.
  • A trader with a real edge of 1% a trade keeps most of it. 27 bps is then about a quarter.
  • Larger orders dilute the US$0.99 per-order fee, though not the stamp duty.
  • During the 180-day promo the cost is about 21 bps.
  • This is a snapshot of one day, and I tested no strategy. It is cost arithmetic and not a backtest.
  • Jika duti setem hanya dikenakan pada satu belah, satu pusingan turun kepada kira-kira 17 bps.
  • Peniaga yang mempunyai kelebihan sebenar 1% setiap dagangan menyimpan sebahagian besarnya. 27 bps ialah kira-kira suku.
  • Pesanan lebih besar mencairkan yuran US$0.99 setiap pesanan, tetapi tidak duti setem.
  • Semasa promosi 180 hari, kos ialah kira-kira 21 bps.
  • Ini gambaran satu hari, dan saya tidak menguji sebarang strategi. Ia pengiraan kos dan bukan ujian belakang.

10A practical planPelan praktikal

1
Pull the fee breakdown of your last 10 US trades from the app.
Ambil pecahan yuran 10 dagangan AS terakhir anda daripada aplikasi.
Measure: total cost per round trip in bps.Ukuran: jumlah kos setiap pusingan dalam bps.
2
Work out your break-even: round-trip cost divided by the ATR % of the stock you trade.
Kira titik pulang modal anda: kos satu pusingan dibahagi ATR % saham yang anda dagang.
Measure: break-even below 10% of the range.Ukuran: pulang modal di bawah 10% daripada julat.
3
Paper trade 20 sessions with the full cost model switched on.
Berdagang kertas selama 20 sesi dengan model kos penuh diaktifkan.
Measure: net result after costs, not before.Ukuran: keputusan bersih selepas kos, bukan sebelumnya.
4
If you use leverage, cap it so that one ATR move is under 10% of your equity.
Jika menggunakan leveraj, hadkan supaya satu pergerakan ATR kurang daripada 10% ekuiti anda.
Measure: leverage x ATR % at or below 10 (about 2.5x at a 4% ATR).Ukuran: leveraj x ATR % pada atau di bawah 10 (kira-kira 2.5x pada ATR 4%).

My takePandangan saya

The range in these stocks is large enough. The hurdle is the 27 bps you pay on every trade, and most of it is stamp duty. Fewer, larger-target trades fit this fee structure. Many small scalps do not.

Julat saham ini cukup besar. Halangannya ialah 27 bps yang dibayar pada setiap dagangan, dan kebanyakannya duti setem. Dagangan yang lebih sedikit dengan sasaran lebih besar sesuai dengan struktur yuran ini. Banyak skalp kecil tidak sesuai.
