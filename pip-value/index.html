Satu pip bernilai berapa? Jawapannya menentukan risiko andaWhat is one pip worth? The answer sets your risk

Pip hanyalah unit. Yang penting ialah nilainya dalam wang, kerana itulah yang menentukan saiz kerugian anda.A pip is just a unit. What matters is what it is worth in money, because that sets the size of your loss.

Ringkasan pantas
  • 1 pip = 0.0001 bagi kebanyakan pasangan, 0.01 bagi pasangan JPY.
  • Nilai satu pip bergantung pada 3 perkara: pasangan, saiz lot dan mata wang akaun.
  • EUR/USD, akaun USD: 1.00 lot ≈ $10 sepip, 0.10 lot ≈ $1, 0.01 lot ≈ $0.10.
  • Henti rugi 20 pip boleh bermaksud rugi $20 atau $200. Saiz lot yang membezakannya.
  • Kira saiz lot daripada risiko dalam wang, bukan daripada perasaan.
The short version
  • 1 pip = 0.0001 for most pairs, 0.01 for JPY pairs.
  • A pip's value depends on 3 things: the pair, your lot size and your account currency.
  • EUR/USD, USD account: 1.00 lot ≈ $10 per pip, 0.10 lot ≈ $1, 0.01 lot ≈ $0.10.
  • A 20-pip stop can mean a $20 or a $200 loss. Lot size makes the difference.
  • Size the lot from risk in money, not from feel.

Kebanyakan peniaga baharu belajar "1 pip = 0.0001" lalu berhenti di situ. Itu bahagian yang mudah.

Bahagian yang menentukan risiko ialah nilai satu pip dalam wang. Dua peniaga boleh memakai henti rugi yang sama, tetapi menanggung risiko yang jauh berbeza.

Most new traders learn "1 pip = 0.0001" and stop there. That is the easy part.

The part that decides your risk is what one pip is worth in money. Two traders can use the same stop and carry very different risk.

01

Pip ialah unit, bukan wangA pip is a unit, not money

Pip ialah langkah harga yang terkecil dalam sebut harga biasa. Pada EUR/USD, pergerakan dari 1.0850 ke 1.0851 ialah satu pip.

Pasangan JPY menggunakan tempat perpuluhan kedua, jadi satu pip ialah 0.01. USD/JPY dari 150.00 ke 150.01 ialah satu pip.

A pip is the smallest standard step in a price quote. On EUR/USD, a move from 1.0850 to 1.0851 is one pip.

JPY pairs use the second decimal place, so one pip is 0.01. USD/JPY moving from 150.00 to 150.01 is one pip.

03

Pip sama, risiko berbezaSame pips, different risk

Dua peniaga memakai henti rugi 20 pip. Seorang berdagang 0.10 lot. Seorang lagi berdagang 1.00 lot.

Pip sama. Kerugian jika terkena henti rugi: $20 berbanding $200.

Two traders both use a 20-pip stop. One trades 0.10 lot. The other trades 1.00 lot.

Same pips. Loss if the stop is hit: $20 versus $200.

04

Tentukan saiz lot daripada risikoSize the lot from risk

Mulakan dengan wang yang sanggup anda rugi, kemudian bina saiz lot daripadanya.

Start with the money you are willing to lose, then build the lot size from it.

Saiz lot = risiko ($) ÷ (henti rugi dalam pip × nilai sepip bagi 1 lot)Lot size = risk ($) ÷ (stop in pips × value per pip per 1 lot)
Contoh kerjaWorked example
Akaun $10,000, risiko 1% = $100, henti rugi 20 pip, nilai sepip $10 bagi 1 lot.
$100 ÷ (20 × $10) = 0.50 lot.
Account $10,000, risk 1% = $100, stop 20 pips, pip value $10 per 1 lot.
$100 ÷ (20 × $10) = 0.50 lot.

Jika henti rugi dilebarkan ke 40 pip, saiz lot menjadi separuh iaitu 0.25. Risiko kekal $100.

Widen the stop to 40 pips and the lot size halves to 0.25. The risk stays at $100.

05

Tiga semakan sebelum setiap daganganThree checks before every trade

Senarai semak
  1. Tahu henti rugi anda dalam pip.
  2. Tahu nilai sepip bagi pasangan itu.
  3. Kira kerugian dalam wang jika henti rugi terkena.
Checklist
  1. Know your stop in pips.
  2. Know the pip value for that pair.
  3. Work out the dollar loss if the stop is hit.

Jika anda tidak dapat menyatakan kerugian dalam wang sebelum masuk dagangan, saiz posisi terlalu besar atau pelan belum jelas.

If you cannot state the dollar loss before entering, the position is too big or the plan is unclear.

Andaian dan notaAssumptions and notes
  • Semua contoh menganggap akaun USD dan 1.00 lot = 100,000 unit, iaitu saiz kontrak forex standard. Broker anda mungkin berbeza.
  • Nilai sepip bagi pasangan USD asas dikira sebagai 10 ÷ harga (USD/CHF, USD/CAD). Bagi USD/JPY, 1,000 ÷ harga.
  • Angka adalah anggaran untuk pendidikan. Sahkan dengan spesifikasi kontrak dan kalkulator broker anda.
  • All examples assume a USD account and 1.00 lot = 100,000 units, the standard forex contract size. Your broker may differ.
  • Pip value for USD-base pairs is worked as 10 ÷ price (USD/CHF, USD/CAD). For USD/JPY, 1,000 ÷ price.
  • Figures are approximate and for education. Verify against your broker's contract specification and calculator.
