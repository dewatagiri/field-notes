What AHB pays, what stands behind it, and what RM300 a month could become in 10 years.Apa yang AHB bayar, apa yang menyokongnya, dan apa jadi kepada RM300 sebulan selepas 10 tahun.

By Azman Taufik · November 2026 · Personal research noteOleh Azman Taufik · November 2026 · Nota kajian peribadi

I attended a Pelaburan Hartanah Berhad (PHB) event today and heard how Amanah Hartanah Bumiputera works. Credit to PHB for opening a door to property ownership for Bumiputera savers. It made me curious, so I checked the numbers and set AHB beside REITs, EPF, ASB, Tabung Haji and fixed deposits.

Hari ini saya berpeluang menghadiri satu majlis Pelaburan Hartanah Berhad (PHB) dan mendengar cara Amanah Hartanah Bumiputera berfungsi. Syabas kepada PHB kerana membuka pintu pemilikan hartanah kepada penabung Bumiputera. Ia buat saya ingin tahu, jadi saya semak angka dan letakkan AHB bersebelahan REIT, KWSP, ASB, Tabung Haji dan simpanan tetap.

The answer firstJawapan dahulu

  • AHB paid 5.00 sen per unit for FY2026, the fifth year in a row at that level. Net of tax and zakat.
  • RM300 a month for 10 years (RM36,000 in) grows to about RM46,000 at 5%, or about RM44,000 without the sponsor bonus.
  • It is regulated as a unit trust, not a REIT. That means a fixed RM1 price, no capital gain, and a sponsor behind the income.
  • It is not a free lunch. Payouts are lower than 2012–2018, and part of the 5 sen is sponsor-funded.
  • Against the field: about double a fixed deposit, below EPF and ASB, and a similar yield to Axis REIT but with no price swings and no growth.
  • AHB agih 5.00 sen seunit bagi FY2026, tahun kelima berturut-turut pada kadar ini. Bersih cukai dan zakat.
  • RM300 sebulan selama 10 tahun (RM36,000 modal) menjadi lebih kurang RM46,000 pada 5%, atau lebih kurang RM44,000 tanpa bonus penaja.
  • Ia dikawal selia sebagai unit amanah, bukan REIT. Maksudnya harga tetap RM1, tiada keuntungan modal, dan ada penaja di belakang pendapatan.
  • Bukan makan percuma. Agihan lebih rendah berbanding 2012–2018, dan sebahagian daripada 5 sen itu dibiayai penaja.
  • Berbanding yang lain: kira-kira dua kali ganda simpanan tetap, di bawah KWSP dan ASB, dan hasil setara Axis REIT tetapi tanpa turun naik harga dan tanpa pertumbuhan.

1. AHB is not a REIT: same regulator, different deal1. AHB bukan REIT: pengawal selia sama, perjanjian berbeza

Both sit under the Capital Markets and Services Act 2007 and the Securities Commission. The difference is the rulebook. AHB is an unlisted unit trust under the SC’s Guidelines on Unit Trust Funds, with a registered prospectus. A listed REIT follows the SC’s Guidelines on Listed REITs plus Bursa’s listing rules. So a REIT unit trades at a market price that moves daily. An AHB unit is bought and sold at a fixed RM1 and the fund does not share in property capital gains. In return, the unitholder gets a steadier ride, with the sponsor (PHB) leasing back the properties and topping up income.

Kedua-duanya berada di bawah Akta Pasaran Modal dan Perkhidmatan 2007 dan Suruhanjaya Sekuriti. Bezanya buku peraturan. AHB ialah unit amanah tidak tersenarai di bawah Garis Panduan Dana Unit Amanah SC, dengan prospektus berdaftar. REIT tersenarai mengikut Garis Panduan REIT Tersenarai SC serta peraturan penyenaraian Bursa. Jadi unit REIT didagangkan pada harga pasaran yang bergerak setiap hari. Unit AHB dibeli dan dijual pada RM1 tetap dan dana tidak menikmati keuntungan modal hartanah. Sebagai ganti, pemegang unit mendapat perjalanan yang lebih stabil, dengan penaja (PHB) menyewa balik hartanah dan menokok pendapatan.

Strengths of eachKekuatan masing-masing

AHB: stable price, steady income, low entry at RM100, access built for Bumiputera. REIT: open market pricing, can be sold any trading day, and rents and asset values can grow. The trade-off is simple: AHB gives calm, a REIT gives upside and swings.

AHB: harga stabil, pendapatan tetap, modal permulaan rendah RM100, akses dibina untuk Bumiputera. REIT: harga pasaran terbuka, boleh dijual pada hari dagangan, sewa dan nilai aset boleh meningkat. Pertukarannya mudah: AHB memberi ketenangan, REIT memberi potensi naik dan turun.

The “not geared” point is my reading of the lease-back structure; check the prospectus before quoting it.

Perkara “tidak berhutang” ialah bacaan saya terhadap struktur sewa balik; semak prospektus sebelum memetiknya.

2. The wider field: REITs, EPF, ASB, Tabung Haji and fixed deposits2. Gambaran lebih luas: REIT, KWSP, ASB, Tabung Haji dan simpanan tetap

Same question, different answers. The bases differ (net, gross, forecast), so read each label before comparing.

Soalan sama, jawapan berbeza. Asas pengiraan berbeza (bersih, kasar, ramalan), jadi baca label sebelum membandingkan.

AHB FY2026 and Tabung Haji 2025 are net of tax and zakat. REIT figures are gross yields: Axis on its RM1.92 year-end 2025 price, the others Kenanga forecasts for June 2026 as reported by StashAway. Reading the fieldMembaca perbandingan

  • Fixed deposit: the policy rate (OPR) is 2.75% and inflation was about 1.6% in early 2026. AHB pays about double the board FD rate, but FD has PIDM protection and AHB does not.
  • EPF and ASB: both pay more (6.15% for 2025; 5.75% ASB). EPF is retirement savings you cannot freely withdraw, so it is not a like-for-like choice.
  • Tabung Haji: 3.50% for 2025, after zakat.
  • Large REITs: forecast gross yields of roughly 4.9–6.6%. The unit price moves every day, and the yield moves with it.
  • Simpanan tetap: kadar dasar (OPR) 2.75% dan inflasi sekitar 1.6% awal 2026. AHB bayar kira-kira dua kali ganda kadar papan FD, tetapi FD dilindungi PIDM dan AHB tidak.
  • KWSP dan ASB: kedua-duanya bayar lebih (6.15% bagi 2025; ASB 5.75%). KWSP ialah simpanan persaraan yang tidak boleh dikeluarkan sesuka hati, jadi bukan pilihan setara.
  • Tabung Haji: 3.50% bagi 2025, selepas zakat.
  • REIT besar: ramalan hasil kasar sekitar 4.9–6.6%. Harga unit bergerak setiap hari, dan hasil bergerak bersamanya.

Harga didagangkan antara RM1.71 dan RM2.20 pada 2025, jadi paras tertinggi kira-kira 29% di atas terendah. Unit AHB kekal RM1.00.

Where AHB fitsDi mana AHB sesuai

Axis paid a similar yield, and its payout grew about 11% from 2021 to 2025 (9.49 to 10.55 sen). But 2026 is softer: the 1H26 DPU was 4.8 sen, and the Q2 payout of 2.3 sen was below 2.65 sen a year earlier. A REIT can rise and fall. AHB trades growth for a flat RM1 price. Pick by what you need: calm income, or income with upside and swings.

Axis membayar hasil yang serupa, dan agihannya naik kira-kira 11% dari 2021 ke 2025 (9.49 ke 10.55 sen). Tetapi 2026 lebih lembut: DPU 1H26 ialah 4.8 sen, dan agihan S2 sebanyak 2.3 sen lebih rendah daripada 2.65 sen setahun lalu. REIT boleh naik dan turun. AHB menukar pertumbuhan dengan harga RM1 yang rata. Pilih ikut keperluan: pendapatan tenang, atau pendapatan dengan potensi naik dan turun.

3. What it has paid over time3. Rekod agihan dari tahun ke tahun

AHB started at 5.42 sen in FY2011, peaked at 6.60 sen in FY2014–2015, slid to 4.70 sen in FY2021, then settled at 5.00 sen. The fund then ran the same direction as its better-known peer, ASB, but at a lower level in every year where I have both numbers.

AHB bermula dengan 5.42 sen pada FY2011, mencapai puncak 6.60 sen pada FY2014–2015, jatuh ke 4.70 sen pada FY2021, kemudian tetap pada 5.00 sen. Arahnya sama dengan ASB yang lebih dikenali, tetapi lebih rendah bagi setiap tahun yang saya ada kedua-dua angka.

4. What stands behind the unit4. Apa yang menyokong unit anda

Unitholders hold beneficial ownership of commercial properties that PHB leases back. Healthcare and offices lead the list, with newer industrial buys in Kulim and near Tanjung Pelepas.

Pemegang unit memegang pemilikan benefisial hartanah komersial yang disewa balik oleh PHB. Report 1/26 says PHB largely met its mandate. Laporan 1/26 menyatakan PHB sebahagian besarnya memenuhi mandat. Ini mengehadkan kelajuan PHB menerbitkan unit baharu.

5. The RM300 a month challenge5. Cabaran RM300 sebulan

A young Bumiputera starts work and puts RM300 into AHB every month for 10 years. Distributions are reinvested as units, which is how AHB pays holders under 10,000 units.

Seorang anak muda Bumiputera mula bekerja dan melabur RM300 sebulan dalam AHB selama 10 tahun. Agihan dilaburkan semula sebagai unit, seperti cara AHB membayar pemegang di bawah 10,000 unit.

6. Before you start: five checks6. Sebelum mula: lima semakan

  • Not a government guarantee. The RM1 price is supported by PHB’s structure, not a sovereign guarantee.
  • Bonus is not promised. It is declared each period by the sponsor.
  • No capital growth. Price stays at RM1, so you earn income only.
  • Subscription comes in tranches. Units are issued when PHB injects assets, so supply is limited.
  • Compare with ASB first. Where I have both, ASB paid more. AHB is a way to add Bumiputera savings beyond that limit, in property rather than equities.
  • Bukan jaminan kerajaan. Harga RM1 disokong struktur PHB, bukan jaminan kerajaan.
  • Bonus tidak dijanjikan. Ia diisytiharkan penaja setiap tempoh.
  • Tiada pertumbuhan modal. Harga kekal RM1, jadi anda hanya dapat pendapatan.
  • Langganan dibuka secara berperingkat. Unit diterbitkan apabila PHB menyuntik aset, jadi bekalan terhad.
  • Banding dengan ASB dahulu. Di mana saya ada kedua-dua angka, ASB bayar lebih. AHB ialah cara menambah simpanan Bumiputera melebihi had itu, dalam hartanah dan bukan ekuiti.

The challengeCabarannya

Do your own research. Read the prospectus. Ask the questions in this note. If it fits, start with RM100 and build the habit.

Buat kajian sendiri. Baca prospektus. Tanya soalan dalam nota ini. Kalau sesuai, mulakan dengan RM100 dan bina tabiat.
