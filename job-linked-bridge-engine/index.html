Jika pelanggan tidak membayar, siapa menanggung kerugian?If the customer does not pay, who takes the loss?

Saya bina satu enjin yang mengikut peraturan pinjaman jambatan terikat kerja, kemudian mengujinya. Ini hasilnya, dalam bahasa yang mudah.I built an engine that follows the rules of a job-linked bridge loan, then tested it. Here is what it showed, in plain words.

Ringkasan pantasThe short version
  • Kedai bahan dibayar, bukan tukang. Wang pinjaman tidak pernah masuk ke tangan tukang.The shop gets paid, not the tradesman. The loan money never reaches his hands.
  • Jika pelanggan tidak membayar, tiga pihak menanggung kerugian mengikut turutan: rizab penaja, penjamin, kemudian pembiaya.If the customer does not pay, three parties take the loss in order: the sponsor's reserve, the guarantor, then the lender.
  • Dalam ujian komputer, perkara yang paling menentukan ialah berapa banyak wang berjaya dikutip semula apabila ada masalah.In the computer test, the biggest factor was how much money is recovered when something goes wrong.
  • Yuran yang adil dikira daripada kos sebenar, bukan ditebak.A fair fee is worked out from real costs, not guessed.

01Kisah seorang tukang catA painter's story

Seorang tukang cat mendapat kerja bernilai RM12,000. Pelanggan membayar deposit RM2,000. Cat dan bahan lain berharga RM5,000, dan kedai mahu dibayar sebelum menghantar. Bank meminta cagaran. Dia tidak mempunyainya. Siapa menampung jurang itu?A painter wins a RM12,000 job. The customer pays a RM2,000 deposit. Paint and other materials cost RM5,000, and the shop wants payment before it delivers. A bank asks for security. He has none. Who covers the gap?

RM12,000nilai kerjathe job
RM2,000deposit daripada pelanggandeposit from the customer
RM5,000kos cat dan bahancost of paint and materials
RM3,000jurang yang perlu ditampungthe gap to cover
RM5,000 − RM2,000 = RM3,000RM5,000 − RM2,000 = RM3,000Itulah jumlah pinjaman. Tidak lebih daripada jurang itu.That is the loan. Never more than the gap.

02Ke mana wang pergi?Where does the money go?

1
Pelanggan mengesahkan kerja dan membayar deposit.The customer confirms the job and pays the deposit.
2
Pembiaya membayar RM3,000 terus kepada kedai bahan.The lender pays the RM3,000 straight to the materials shop.
3
Tukang menyiapkan kerja.The painter finishes the job.
4
Pelanggan membayar baki ke satu akaun yang dikawal pembiaya.The customer pays the balance into an account the lender controls.
5
Pinjaman dilunaskan dahulu. Bakinya dilepaskan kepada tukang.The loan is repaid first. The rest goes to the painter.
WANG TIDAK PERNAH MASUK AKAUN TUKANG.THE MONEY NEVER ENTERS THE PAINTER'S ACCOUNT. Kedai dibayar terus. Dengan cara ini, pinjaman tidak boleh dibelanjakan untuk perkara lain.The shop is paid directly. So the loan cannot be spent on anything else.

03Siapa yang layak?Who qualifies?

Empat soalan. Keempat-empatnya mesti dijawab ya.Four questions. All four must be yes.

1

Ada kerja yang sah dan pembayar bernama, yang bukan tukang itu sendiri?Is there a real job with a named payer who is not the tradesman himself?

2

Kos utama ialah bahan yang boleh dibeli, seperti cat, jubin atau wayar?Is the main cost something you can buy, like paint, tiles or wire?

3

Pembayar ialah isi rumah, individu atau perniagaan kecil?Is the payer a household, a person or a small business?

4

Deposit kurang daripada kos bahan? Jika tidak, pinjaman tidak diperlukan.Is the deposit less than the cost of materials? If not, no loan is needed.

Selepas itu, ada semakan lain: pengesahan identiti, saringan jenayah kewangan, rekod kredit, dan kedai bahan mesti dalam senarai yang diluluskan. Jika pegawai perlu menyemak, pegawai yang menentukan. Enjin hanya mencadangkan.Then come more checks: proof of identity, financial-crime screening, credit record, and the shop must be on an approved list. If a person needs to review, the person decides. The engine only recommends.
