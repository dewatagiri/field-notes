Ringkasan pantasThe short version
  • Peraturan menjadi kod. 15 bahagian kecil, 2,678 baris Python, 141 ujian automatik. Setiap had dan kadar ialah tetapan, bukan angka tersembunyi dalam kod.The rules became code. 15 small parts, 2,678 lines of Python, 141 automatic tests. Every limit and rate is a setting, not a number hidden in the code.
  • Satu kerja masuk, satu keputusan keluar, lengkap dengan sebabnya.One job goes in, one decision comes out, with the reasons.
  • Satu kumpulan pinjaman dimainkan 1,000 kali untuk melihat siapa menanggung kerugian dan siapa untung.One group of loans is played out 1,000 times to see who takes the loss and who profits.
  • Keputusan: kadar kutipan semula paling menentukan, dan rizab habis dalam 36% kumpulan.The result: recovery matters most, and the reserve ran out in 36% of groups.

02Logiknya dalam tujuh langkahThe logic in seven steps

Contohnya tetap tukang cat tadi: kerja RM12,000, deposit RM2,000, bahan RM5,000. Setiap langkah ada satu peraturan dan satu jawapan.The example is still the painter: a RM12,000 job, a RM2,000 deposit, RM5,000 of materials. Each step has one rule and one answer.

1Empat soalanFour questionsscreen.py
Ada kerja sah dan pembayar bernama? Kos utama ialah bahan? Pembayar layak? Deposit kurang daripada kos bahan? Satu jawapan tidak, dan proses berhenti di sini.A real job and a named payer? Is the main cost materials? Is the payer eligible? Is the deposit below the materials cost? One no, and the process stops here.
Tukang cat: ya, ya, ya, ya. Lulus.Painter: yes, yes, yes, yes. Passed.
2SemakanChecksdecide.py
Pengesahan identiti, saringan jenayah kewangan, identiti pembayar, rekod kredit, pendaftaran perniagaan, rekod bidang, persetujuan data peribadi, kedai dalam senarai, satu pinjaman aktif bagi satu kerja. Hasilnya: tolak, sekat, atau teruskan.Identity, financial-crime screening, payer identity, credit record, business registration, trade record, data consent, shop on the list, one live loan per job. The result: decline, block, or carry on.
Semua lulus.All passed.
3Berapa banyakHow muchdecide.py
Kos bahan tolak deposit, dihadkan oleh peratusan kos bahan yang dibenarkan, had setiap pinjaman, invois kedai, dan had bagi peminjam, kedai, kumpulan dan perintis. Tidak pernah melebihi kos bahan.Materials cost minus the deposit, capped by the allowed share of materials, the per-loan limit, the shop's invoice, and the limits for the borrower, the shop, the group and the pilot. Never above the materials cost.
RM5,000 − RM2,000 = RM3,000 (60% kos bahan)RM5,000 − RM2,000 = RM3,000 (60% of materials)
4Bayar siapa, siapa lulusPay whom, who approvesdecide.py
Wang pergi kepada kedai, bukan tukang. Kerja kecil yang sepadan dengan templat diluluskan automatik. Kerja besar atau tidak sepadan pergi kepada pegawai.The money goes to the shop, not the painter. A small job that matches the template is approved automatically. A large or unusual one goes to an officer.
Bayar RM3,000 kepada kedai. Lulus automatik.Pay RM3,000 to the shop. Approved automatically.

5Apa jadi kemudianWhat happens nextsimulate.py
Setiap pinjaman berakhir dengan satu daripada lima keputusan. Kebarangkaliannya ialah tetapan, dan di sini semuanya rekaan.Every loan ends in one of five ways. The chances are settings, and here they are all made up.
80%bayar tepat masapays on time
10%lewat bayarpays late
5%bayar tunai kepada tukangpays painter in cash
3%pertikaiandispute
2%tukang tinggalkan kerjapainter walks away
6Berapa yang dapat dikutip semulaHow much comes backcollections.py
Setiap keputusan ada kadar kutipan semula: tepat masa 100%, lewat 95%, tunai kepada tukang 50%, pertikaian 70%, tinggal kerja 30% (rekaan). Kerugian = jumlah pinjaman × (1 − kadar kutipan).Each ending has a recovery rate: on time 100%, late 95%, cash to the painter 50%, dispute 70%, walked away 30% (made up). Loss = loan amount × (1 − recovery rate).
Pinjaman RM3,000, pelanggan bayar tunai kepada tukang: kerugian RM1,500.A RM3,000 loan where the customer pays the painter in cash: a RM1,500 loss.
7Siapa menanggung kerugianWho takes the losswaterfall.py
Rizab penaja (RM10,000 bagi satu kumpulan) habis dahulu. Selepas itu penjamin menanggung 50% sehingga RM5,000. Pembiaya menanggung bakinya.The sponsor's reserve (RM10,000 per group) goes first. Then the guarantor takes 50% up to RM5,000. The lender takes the rest.
Kerugian RM25,000: rizab RM10,000, penjamin RM5,000, pembiaya RM10,000.A RM25,000 loss: reserve RM10,000, guarantor RM5,000, lender RM10,000.
Ilustrasi, bukan data.Illustration, not data.

05Apa yang menggerakkan hasilWhat moves the result

Untuk tahu apa yang penting, enjin menukar satu perkara sebanyak suku pada satu masa, dengan nasib yang sama, dan melihat hasil setiap pinjaman.To find out what matters, the engine changes one thing by a quarter at a time, with the same luck, and watches the result per loan.

Untung setiap pinjaman jika satu perkara menjadi lebih burukProfit per loan when one thing gets worse

Untung asal RM25 setiap pinjaman. Angka rekaan.Starting profit RM25 per loan. Made-up numbers.
Kutipan semula turun suku: untung RM25 menjadi −RM22Recovery down a quarter: profit RM25 becomes −RM22Setiap pinjaman. Tiada perkara lain menggoncang hasil sebanyak ini.On every loan. Nothing else moved the result this much.

1. Kutipan semula1. Recovery

Apabila pinjaman bermasalah, berapa wang kembali? Itu yang kita belum tahu di Malaysia.When a loan goes wrong, how much money comes back? That is what we do not yet know in Malaysia.

2. Had pinjaman bukan tuasnya2. The loan limit is not the lever

Deposit menentukan saiz pinjaman. Menaikkan had melebihi 90% kos bahan tidak mengubah apa-apa.The deposit sets the loan size. Raising the limit above 90% of materials changed nothing.

3. Yuran mesti dikira3. Work the fee out

Yuran RM60 yang saya reka kira-kira 2.2% sebulan. Yuran yang cukup menampung kos dan jangkaan kerugian ialah kira-kira RM35 (1.4% sebulan).The RM60 fee I made up is about 2.2% a month. A fee that covers costs and expected loss is about RM35 (1.4% a month).

4. Rizab diuji dengan data4. Test the reserve with data

Saiz rizab yang betul hanya boleh diketahui daripada kutipan semula sebenar.The right reserve size can only come from real recovery data.

Pandangan sayaMy takeDengan anggaran ini produk tidak berfungsi pada 5% bayaran tunai. Ia mula berfungsi di bawah kira-kira 1%. Maknanya kawalan akaun, bukan harga, yang menentukan sama ada pinjaman kecil seperti ini boleh wujud. Ini sengaja harga pembangunan: ia rugi dengan sengaja, dan nilainya diukur pada pekerjaan dan tambahan (additionality).On these assumptions the product does not work at 5% cash payments. It starts to work below about 1%. So controlling the account, not the price, decides whether small loans like this can exist. This is developmental pricing on purpose: it knowingly loses money, and its value is measured in jobs and additionality.

08Apa yang belum diketahuiWhat we do not know yet

PerhatianWatch out
  • Tiada data kerugian Malaysia. Semua kebarangkalian dan kadar kutipan ialah rekaan.No Malaysian loss data exists. Every probability and recovery rate is made up.
  • Kira-kira 30 tetapan perlu dipersetujui pembiaya, penjamin dan penaja sebelum ada pinjaman sebenar.About 30 settings must be agreed by the lender, guarantor and sponsor before any real loan.
  • Simulasi menganggap setiap pinjaman berdiri sendiri, dan yuran atau had tidak mengubah tingkah laku orang. Dunia sebenar tidak begitu.The simulation treats each loan as independent, and assumes fees and limits do not change how people behave. The real world is not like that.
