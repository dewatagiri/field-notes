Friedman or Maslahah: whose rules does Malaysian business run on?

Company law still sits on the shareholder side. The language and the financial system lean towards Maqasid. The work of protecting workers and the public is being done mostly by the state, not by boards.

The short version
  • Company law still follows the shareholder line. Section 213 of the Companies Act 2016 is about the company's best interests, and the MCCG is comply-or-explain.
  • Finance leans Maqasid on scale: Islamic financing was 48% of total financing in 2025. On outcomes it is weaker. The BNM governor says financing for priority sectors is still modest.
  • The state, not the board, carries the stakeholder load: a RM2,000 minimum wage from June 2027 (firms with sales below RM50 million exempt), the Gig Workers Act, targeted fuel subsidies.
  • Employee pay is 33.9% of GDP against a 40% target for 2030. By my calculation the target needs about 3.8 times the 2025 pace.
  • Data centres are the live test: about 7% of Peninsular electricity today, up to 31% by 2035 on a ministry projection.

Who does a Malaysian company answer to? In law, mostly its shareholders. In public language, everyone: workers, customers, the community, even the next generation. In 2026 the gap between those two answers is easier to measure than it used to be.

Milton Friedman wrote in 1970 that a manager's job is to make money for the owners within the rules of society. Islamic commercial ethics, read through Maqasid al-Shariah and Maslahah, asks for profit that serves public welfare and shares risk. Malaysia uses both vocabularies at once.

This note tests each against what the country is doing now: company law, the banks, Budget 2027 and the data-centre boom. Then it asks where 2030 lands.

01 · What Friedman actually said

His 1970 essay is shorter and more careful than its reputation. The manager is the owners' employee, and the job is to make money for them. But he wrote that this must be done inside the basic rules of society, which he defined as both law and ethical custom.

That clause matters here. If Islamic ethics is part of Malaysia's ethical custom, Friedman and Maslahah overlap more than the usual debate suggests. The real disagreement sits on three tests.

02 · What Maqasid asks of business

Maqasid al-Shariah are the higher objectives of Islamic law. In commerce they show up as public welfare (Maslahah ‘Ammah), trusteeship over wealth (Amanah), justice and generosity (‘Adl and Ihsan), and the principle that gain comes with risk. The legal maxim against inflicting or returning harm is the one that bites on externalities.

These are not only private beliefs in Malaysia. Bank Negara says Islamic finance is built on the objectives of Shariah, and that Value-Based Intermediation (VBI) is how those values are put into practice. In 2024, Islamic financial institutions directed RM148.6 billion into VBI-aligned activities.

03 · Law and finance today

Section 213 requires directors to act in good faith, for a proper purpose, in the best interest of the company. Commentators note that, unlike section 172 of the UK Companies Act 2006, it does not name wider stakeholders. A breach can mean a fine up to RM3 million, up to five years in jail, or both. The MCCG asks listed companies to follow its principles or explain why not.

Disclosure is tightening. Under the National Sustainability Reporting Framework, the largest listed companies must apply all ISSB requirements from 1 January 2027. Reporting is a step towards stakeholder regard, but it is not yet a duty to act on it.

Finance is where Malaysia looks most like Maqasid. Islamic financing rose from 25% of total financing in 2015 to 48% by 2025, and the Islamic capital market is above 60% of the domestic capital market. Scale is not the same as outcome, though. In September the BNM governor said financing for priority sectors is still modest, with approvals concentrated in services. He put it down to information gaps, complex structuring and limited sector familiarity, not unwilling banks.

Trap: the label is not the outcome

Critics report that common Islamic home-financing structures in Malaysia leave buyers carrying the risk when a project stalls. That is the opposite of risk-sharing. A 48% share tells us about market share. It does not tell us whether Maqasid outcomes are being delivered.

04 · The state is doing the stakeholder work

  • Minimum wage: RM1,700 to RM2,000 from June 2027, covering more than four million workers. SMEs with annual sales below RM50 million are exempt. No end date for the exemption was given.
  • Gig workers: the Gig Workers Act took effect on 31 March 2026 and covers about 1.64 million people. A council is due to set minimum earnings by early 2027.
  • Subsidies: targeted RON95 subsidies are expected to save RM2.5 to 4 billion a year.
  • SME financing: financing and guarantee facilities for MSMEs and mid-sized firms go to RM57 billion in 2027, from RM50 billion this year.

The pressure on firms arrives as regulation. So firms do what Friedman said: they follow the rules of the game. The ethic comes in as law, not as a board duty.

The state has limits. The fiscal deficit target is 3.3% of GDP in 2027, with federal debt at 63.7% against a 60% cap in the fiscal responsibility framework. An election is due by February 2028. Reversals in an election year are a real risk.

05 · The number that decides it

Employee pay was RM686.9 billion in 2025, a record, but its share of GDP rose only from 33.6% to 33.9%. The business operating surplus took 62%. The government wants employee pay at 40% of GDP by 2030.

My calculation
  • Reaching 40% from 33.9% in five years needs about +1.2 points a year. The 2025 gain was +0.3.
  • Employee pay must grow about 3.4 points a year faster than nominal GDP. In 2025 it grew about 0.9 points faster. That is roughly 3.8 times the pace.
  • At the 2025 pace the share reaches about 35.4% in 2030.
  • Assumption: a straight line. Wage-share gains could bunch up after the June 2027 minimum-wage rise, so the path may bend.
Two targets, one question

The Budget 2026 economy chapter lists a 45% labour-income goal under the Madani economy framework. The 13th Malaysia Plan milestone is 40% by 2030. I have not checked whether these differ only in time horizon.

Data centres are where Friedman's problem shows up in plain view. The profit is private. The water and power come from the shared grid.

Bank Negara estimates that a 50MW data centre uses as much water as 2,200 households a day and as much electricity as 22,000. Johor has halted approvals for Tier-1 and Tier-2 data centres over water use. The government plans “user-pays” electricity pricing for large consumers and has said it will not offer cheap power and water to operators.

This is the Maslahah rule against harm, applied by regulators because the firms have not been asked to apply it themselves.

The bear case, in its strongest form

Friedman's side has a real reply. Malaysia is growing under this hybrid: 2026 growth is projected at 4.8 to 5.3%, and the country rose to 15th in the IMD competitiveness ranking. There is no evidence yet that the stakeholder push has cost growth. Pressure from outside also runs the other way: commentators in the US have argued that shareholder primacy is back and that stakeholder capitalism went too far. And a wage target missed this widely may only mean it was set too high.

08 · Questions worth asking

For ParliamentShould section 213 name workers, community and environment, as the UK did in section 172?
For the Finance MinistryHow long will the SME minimum-wage exemption last, and what ends it?
For banksWhat share of your financing shares risk with the customer, and what does it deliver in priority sectors?
For listed boardsWhen wage cost and shareholder return conflict, who decides, and is it minuted?
For SME ownersIf the exemption ends, can your margin carry RM2,000 a month per worker?
For investorsDo you ask for water, power and pay data, or only the earnings line?

09 · What I would do

The following are my own analysis, not findings from the sources.

2

3

Call to the Government
  • By Q1 2027: set the review date for the SME minimum-wage exemption.
  • With Budget 2028: publish the pay-share path against the 40% line.
  • By end-2027: open a consultation on whether section 213 should name stakeholders.
BoardsAdd wage, water and power cost to the board agenda. Prepare ISSB statements early if you are in the first group.
BanksReport risk-sharing share and priority-sector results. Build the sector structuring skills the BNM governor asked for.
SMEsPlan for RM2,000 even if exempt today. Ask your financier about guarantee support.
InvestorsAsk for pay and resource-use data, not only earnings.
Self-check for the reader
  • Do I know my firm's pay share of revenue, and has it moved in three years?
  • Which of my costs does someone else pay for today: water, power, land, health?
  • Does my financing share risk with me, or only move it?
  • If the minimum wage reached my firm tomorrow, which line gives first?
  • When did a board I know last discuss a cost that is not on its own books?
My take

Malaysia has not chosen between Friedman and Maslahah. It has split the job. Firms follow the rules, and the state writes them. That works while the state has room to write them. The deficit is 3.3% of GDP and an election is due by February 2028. If pay share stays near 35%, the market will have chosen Friedman by default, whatever the speeches say. If boards start acting on Maslahah before the law forces them to, the gap closes faster and costs less.

  • Dari segi skala, kewangan condong kepada Maqasid: pembiayaan Islam ialah 48% daripada jumlah pembiayaan pada 2025. Dari segi hasil, ia lebih lemah. Gabenor BNM berkata pembiayaan untuk sektor keutamaan masih sederhana.

Kepada siapakah sebuah syarikat Malaysia bertanggungjawab? Dari segi undang-undang, terutamanya kepada pemegang saham. Dari segi bahasa awam, kepada semua orang: pekerja, pelanggan, masyarakat, malah generasi akan datang. Pada 2026, jurang antara dua jawapan itu lebih mudah diukur daripada dahulu.

Pada 1970, Milton Friedman menulis bahawa tugas seorang pengurus ialah menjana wang untuk pemilik dalam lingkungan peraturan masyarakat. Etika muamalat Islam, apabila dibaca melalui Maqasid Syariah dan Maslahah, menuntut keuntungan yang memberi manfaat kepada kepentingan awam dan berkongsi risiko. Malaysia menggunakan kedua-dua bahasa ini serentak.

Nota ini menguji kedua-duanya dengan apa yang sedang berlaku: undang-undang syarikat, bank, Belanjawan 2027 dan lonjakan pusat data. Kemudian ia bertanya di mana kita berada pada 2030.

01 · Apa sebenarnya yang Friedman kata

Esei 1970 itu lebih pendek dan lebih berhati-hati daripada reputasinya. Pengurus ialah pekerja kepada pemilik, dan tugasnya menjana wang untuk mereka. Tetapi beliau menulis bahawa ini mesti dilakukan dalam peraturan asas masyarakat, yang beliau takrifkan sebagai undang-undang dan adat etika.

Syarat itu penting di sini. Jika etika Islam sebahagian daripada adat etika Malaysia, Friedman dan Maslahah bertindih lebih banyak daripada yang disangka. Perbezaan sebenar terletak pada tiga ujian.

02 · Apa yang Maqasid minta daripada perniagaan

Maqasid Syariah ialah tujuan tinggi hukum Islam. Dalam perniagaan, ia muncul sebagai kepentingan awam (Maslahah ‘Ammah), amanah terhadap harta, keadilan dan ihsan (‘Adl dan Ihsan), serta prinsip bahawa keuntungan datang bersama risiko. Kaedah larangan mendatangkan dan membalas mudarat ialah yang paling terkena pada kos yang ditanggung orang lain.

Di Malaysia, ini bukan sekadar pegangan peribadi. Bank Negara menyatakan kewangan Islam dibina atas tujuan Syariah, dan Perantaraan Berasaskan Nilai (VBI) ialah cara nilai itu dilaksanakan. Pada 2024, institusi kewangan Islam menyalurkan RM148.6 bilion ke dalam aktiviti selaras VBI.

03 · Undang-undang dan kewangan hari ini

Seksyen 213 menghendaki pengarah bertindak dengan suci hati, untuk tujuan yang wajar, demi kepentingan terbaik syarikat. Pengulas menyatakan bahawa, tidak seperti seksyen 172 Akta Syarikat UK 2006, ia tidak menyebut pihak berkepentingan yang lebih luas. Pelanggaran boleh membawa denda sehingga RM3 juta, penjara sehingga lima tahun, atau kedua-duanya. MCCG pula meminta syarikat tersenarai mengikut prinsipnya atau menjelaskan sebabnya.

Pendedahan semakin ketat. Di bawah Rangka Kerja Pelaporan Kemampanan Kebangsaan, syarikat tersenarai terbesar mesti menggunakan semua keperluan ISSB mulai 1 Januari 2027. Pelaporan ialah langkah ke arah menimbang pihak berkepentingan, tetapi ia belum menjadi kewajipan untuk bertindak.

Kewangan ialah bidang yang paling hampir dengan Maqasid. Pembiayaan Islam naik daripada 25% jumlah pembiayaan pada 2015 kepada 48% pada 2025, dan pasaran modal Islam melebihi 60% pasaran modal domestik. Skala tidak sama dengan hasil. Pada September, Gabenor BNM berkata pembiayaan untuk sektor keutamaan masih sederhana, dengan kelulusan tertumpu pada sektor perkhidmatan. Beliau menyebut punca seperti jurang maklumat, penstrukturan yang rumit dan kurang kefahaman sektor, bukan bank enggan memberi pinjaman.

Perangkap: label bukan hasil

Pengkritik melaporkan bahawa struktur pembiayaan rumah Islam yang lazim di Malaysia meninggalkan risiko pada pembeli apabila projek terbengkalai. Itu bertentangan dengan perkongsian risiko. Bahagian 48% menunjukkan saiz pasaran. Ia tidak menunjukkan sama ada hasil Maqasid benar-benar sampai.

04 · Kerajaan yang memikul kerja pihak berkepentingan

  • Gaji minimum: RM1,700 naik kepada RM2,000 mulai Jun 2027, melibatkan lebih empat juta pekerja. PMKS dengan jualan tahunan di bawah RM50 juta dikecualikan. Tiada tarikh tamat pengecualian dinyatakan.
  • Pekerja gig: Akta Pekerja Gig 2025 berkuat kuasa pada 31 Mac 2026 dan meliputi kira-kira 1.64 juta orang. Sebuah majlis dijangka menetapkan pendapatan minimum menjelang awal 2027.
  • Subsidi: subsidi RON95 bersasar dijangka menjimatkan RM2.5 hingga 4 bilion setahun.
  • Pembiayaan PMKS: kemudahan pembiayaan dan jaminan untuk PMKS dan syarikat pertengahan meningkat kepada RM57 bilion pada 2027, daripada RM50 bilion tahun ini.

Tekanan ke atas firma datang dalam bentuk peraturan. Maka firma melakukan apa yang Friedman kata: mengikut peraturan permainan. Etika masuk sebagai undang-undang, bukan sebagai tugas lembaga pengarah.

Kerajaan juga ada had. Sasaran defisit fiskal ialah 3.3% daripada KDNK pada 2027, dengan hutang kerajaan persekutuan 63.7% berbanding had 60% dalam kerangka tanggungjawab fiskal. Pilihan raya perlu diadakan menjelang Februari 2028. Pembalikan dasar pada tahun pilihan raya ialah risiko yang nyata.

Hujah menentang, dalam bentuk paling kuat

Pihak Friedman ada jawapan yang kukuh. Malaysia berkembang di bawah campuran ini: pertumbuhan 2026 diunjur 4.8 hingga 5.3%, dan negara naik ke tempat ke-15 dalam kedudukan daya saing IMD. Setakat ini tiada bukti bahawa dorongan memihak pihak berkepentingan menjejaskan pertumbuhan. Tekanan dari luar juga berlawanan arah: pengulas di AS berhujah bahawa keutamaan pemegang saham sudah kembali dan kapitalisme pihak berkepentingan terlalu jauh. Dan sasaran gaji yang tersasar sebegini jauh mungkin sekadar bermakna sasaran itu ditetapkan terlalu tinggi.

09 · Apa yang saya akan buat

Berikut ialah analisis saya sendiri, bukan dapatan daripada sumber.

1
Letakkan tarikh semakan pada pengecualian PMKS. Kelonggaran tanpa tarikh tamat sukar ditarik balik kemudian.Ukuran: peratus pekerja dalam firma dikecualikan yang dibayar kurang RM2,000, dilapor setiap tahun.
2
Terbitkan laluan bahagian gaji bersama setiap Belanjawan. Tunjukkan angka DOSM berbanding garis lurus ke 40%.Ukuran: jurang kepada garis, dalam mata peratusan.
3
Minta bank melaporkan hasil perkongsian risiko di bawah VBI. Bahagian pembiayaan dengan struktur perkongsian risiko atau aset sebenar, dan hasil sektor keutamaan.Ukuran: bahagian yang dilapor setiap tahun, mengikut sektor.
4
Terbitkan penggunaan air dan elektrik pusat data bagi setiap MW. Tapak demi tapak.Ukuran: liter dan MWj bagi setiap MW.
Seruan kepada Kerajaan
  • Menjelang suku pertama 2027: tetapkan tarikh semakan bagi pengecualian gaji minimum PMKS.
  • Bersama Belanjawan 2028: terbitkan laluan bahagian gaji berbanding garis 40%.
  • Menjelang akhir 2027: buka rundingan awam sama ada seksyen 213 perlu menyebut pihak berkepentingan.
Lembaga pengarahTambah kos gaji, air dan elektrik ke dalam agenda lembaga. Sediakan penyata ISSB lebih awal jika anda dalam kumpulan pertama.
BankLaporkan bahagian perkongsian risiko dan hasil sektor keutamaan. Bina kemahiran penstrukturan sektor yang diminta Gabenor BNM.
PMKSRancang untuk RM2,000 walaupun hari ini dikecualikan. Tanya pembiaya anda tentang sokongan jaminan.
PelaburMinta data gaji dan penggunaan sumber, bukan hanya angka keuntungan.
Semak sendiri

  • Kos manakah yang hari ini dibayar orang lain: air, elektrik, tanah, kesihatan?
  • Adakah pembiayaan saya berkongsi risiko dengan saya, atau hanya memindahkannya?
  • Jika gaji minimum sampai ke firma saya esok, baris mana yang tertekan dahulu?
  • Bila kali terakhir sebuah lembaga yang saya kenal membincangkan kos yang tiada dalam bukunya sendiri?
Pandangan saya

Malaysia belum memilih antara Friedman dan Maslahah. Ia membahagikan kerja. Firma mengikut peraturan, kerajaan menulisnya. Itu boleh berjalan selagi kerajaan ada ruang untuk menulisnya. Defisit 3.3% daripada KDNK dan pilihan raya perlu diadakan menjelang Februari 2028. Jika bahagian gaji kekal sekitar 35%, pasaran sebenarnya sudah memilih Friedman secara senyap, walau apa pun yang diucapkan dalam ucapan. Jika lembaga pengarah mula bertindak atas Maslahah sebelum undang-undang memaksa, jurang itu tertutup lebih cepat dan lebih murah.
